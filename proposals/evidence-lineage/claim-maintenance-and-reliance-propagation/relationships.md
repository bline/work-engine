# Relationships: Claim Maintenance and Reliance Propagation

## Split from: claim-centered evidence lineage

Type: causal decomposition preserving the complete parent consequence.

This proposal carries forward the parent's impact, refresh, reliance,
obligation, delivery, concurrency, and recovery semantics. It does not
supersede or narrow the parent and cannot revise the parent's deferred decision.

## Depends on: production claim evidence interface

Type: causal production dependency.

The operational lifecycle requires production stable identities, exact
revisions, domain profiles, authority-bound publication, reliance records, and
truthful projection semantics. Acceptance or implementation must not begin from
the synthetic dogfood as a substitute for that foundation.

## Related to: role-owned durable operational state

Type: reference-only operational consumption.

A role may preserve the exact claim revisions governing its work and pending
obligations. Role state does not become the owner of claim history, judgment,
reliance, or semantic completion.

## Related to: operational state transition history

Type: observable downstream history; non-owning.

Operational history may observe delivery, waiting, recovery, and terminal role
consequences. It cannot infer or publish claim refresh, applicability, reliance,
or completion merely from an operational transition.

## Delivery relationship: control plane and scheduler

Type: reusable transport with separate semantic authority.

A general control plane may deliver stable obligations and the scheduler may
reuse that route for timed presentation. Neither owns the obligation's meaning,
activates an unauthorized role, or converts acknowledgement into completion.
