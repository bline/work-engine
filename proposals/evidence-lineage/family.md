The semantic candidate remains the complete conceptual parent. The first
production candidate deliberately establishes only the stable evidence and
interface substrate. The second preserves and independently forms the remaining
operational lifecycle so that accepting a narrow foundation cannot silently
discard or claim completion of the larger consequence. Neither phase establishes
proposal-research maturity levels, readiness profiles, continuous monitoring,
or organizational compilation. Those remain possible consumers whose own
owners and consequences have not been formed.

## Why this is a separate family

Proposal research claims and specialist review findings need overlapping
evidence semantics but remain different domain judgments. Proposal packets own
proposal identity and current meaning. Research workflows would own research
claims and any profile-specific freshness maintenance they require. Review
workflows would own findings, review episodes, and synthesis. An authorized
role may produce an advisory applicability assessment, while downstream
decision owners decide whether to rely on it and judge readiness for their
exact decisions.

A separate candidate preserves the possibility of a shared semantic primitive
without silently broadening the identity of either the durable-proposal-packet
or revision-bound-review-artifact proposals. It also leaves open the possibility
that dogfooding will prove the overlap too small to justify a shared owner.

## Current evidence and uncertainty

The bootstrap review of the adaptive-specialized-review family identified
bounded subject identity, per-finding addressability, evidence provenance,
assumptions, limitations, lineage, and transition actors as missing semantic
requirements. The research-maturity idea independently needs the same minimum
properties for proposal-research claims, while adding domain-specific maturity,
freshness, and readiness semantics.

The completed bounded dogfood positively demonstrated stable identity,
authority and provenance, non-authoritative impact nomination, both refresh
paths, exact-revision reliance, and typed lineage across its two purposively
selected historical fixtures. That is advisory evidence for the shared
candidate, not evidence that either production proposal's final placement,
schema, or implementation is correct. Real consumers must still establish the
production interface, and the larger adversarial lifecycle and recovery matrix
remains evidence for the dependent operational phase.

## Authority boundary

The dogfood decision approved only its proposal meaning, and its later execution
produced bounded advisory evidence. These artifacts do not establish a
production evidence schema, declare any claim fresh or applicable, assign
unresolved research-maintenance or organizational-planning authority, accept
the parent semantic proposal, change the roadmap, or authorize implementation.
Persistence records each candidate, disposition, and provenance; it does not
increase the authority of the judgments it describes.
