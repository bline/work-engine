# Relationships: Claim-Centered Evidence Lineage

## Conditionally enables: revision-bound review artifacts

Type: conditional owner relationship; the required semantics are causal, but
acceptance of this placement candidate is not.

Review findings need stable statement identity, evidence baselines, provenance,
sensitivity, scoped authority, and non-destructive lineage. The review-artifact
profile adds reviewer episodes, review outcomes, conflicts, and synthesis. If
this candidate is selected as the shared owner it supplies the common
semantics; otherwise the review profile still requires equivalent consequences
from another valid owner.

## Related to: durable proposal packets

Type: neighboring ownership relationship; non-causal.

Proposal packets own proposal identity, current meaning, lifecycle, placement,
uncertainty, relationships, and decision authority. They may reference current
supported evidence or lineage artifacts without absorbing their history or
semantic judgment.

A packet or supporting proposal artifact may rely on exact claim revisions. A
derived view may identify reliance on a candidate-impacted claim, but neither
impact nomination nor claim revision automatically mutates or reopens the
proposal. Its downstream decision owner retains applicability, readiness, and
reopening authority.

## Neighboring producer: implementation completion

Type: candidate-impact producer; non-authoritative for claim semantics.

Implementation completion evidence may produce an independently identified
impact nomination that is `sourced_by` the completion and targets exact claim
revisions through `may_affect`. Completion may identify observed changed files,
symbols, contracts, and baseline transitions and reference consequences
established under separately named authority. An authorized refresh judgment
separately records `reopened_by`, an unchanged refresh, or adjudicated
`changed_because_of` causality. Completion does not acquire research-refresh
authority or authority to characterize claim truth, freshness, applicability,
or causal change.

## Consumer: durable role state

Type: reference-only operational consumption; non-owning.

A role projection may record which exact claim revisions currently govern its
work, the decision-specific assessment on which it relies, and any pending
refresh or selective-reopening obligation. Evidence lineage owns the epistemic
history; role state records the role's current operational reliance and an
integrity-bound reference to any consequence authorized by the owning workflow
or role contract. It neither selects nor authorizes that consequence. Context
replacement must not cause role state to copy or silently advance claim
history.

## Delivery relationship: control plane and scheduler

Type: prospective transport reuse; non-causal for formation.

A claim impact may create a durable domain obligation for a current consumer.
A shared control-plane delivery route may surface it to an active authorized
role. Time-triggered scheduler obligations may reuse the same delivery
machinery, but claim impact is not scheduler-owned scheduling state and neither
route creates activation or refresh authority.

## Projection relationship: claim query index

Type: generated projection; non-owning.

Many-to-many lineage and downstream-reliance queries may require an edge index
or graph-shaped projection. The projection remains rebuildable from canonical
claim, judgment, and relationship artifacts and cannot become evidence for its
own semantic conclusions. Its storage choice is independent of the permanent
semantic owner.

## Potential consumer: proposal research maturity

Type: prospective consumer relationship; not a formed packet relationship.

The originating idea proposes research maturity, facet-specific readiness,
freshness evaluation, and organizational qualification. Those domain semantics
are intentionally not formed inside this candidate. A later proposal may
consume the shared contract if formation establishes an independently decidable
change and a truthful owner.

## Potential consumer: implementation review

Type: prospective consumer relationship; non-causal for formation.

Implementation review may share the same evidence core while requiring a
different subject projection, review episode, remediation lifecycle, and
acceptance boundary. This possibility is an evidence need, not authority to
build a universal substrate now.
