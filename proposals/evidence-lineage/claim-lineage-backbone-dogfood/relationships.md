# Relationships: Claim-Lineage Backbone Dogfood

## Depends on and informs: claim-centered evidence lineage

Type: causal semantic dependency under test plus non-authoritative evidence
feedback.

The dogfood consumes stable claim identity, exact revisions, `may_affect`,
refresh outcomes, `changed_because_of`, and `relies_on` as a hypothesis. Without
those semantics it would be a different experiment. A successful or failed
exercise may inform revision, decomposition, rejection, or later evaluation of
the parent proposal, but it cannot perform those transitions or treat its own
durability as evidence of correctness.

## Informs: revision-bound review artifacts

Type: bounded consumer evidence; non-authoritative for review placement.

One real specialist finding exercises the proposed common evidence core while
the review artifact retains finding, episode, synthesis, severity, conflict,
and outcome semantics. The result may reveal a genuine shared boundary or show
that review needs a different representation. It does not change the existing
review-artifact decision.

## Related to: durable proposal packets

Type: neighboring owner and dogfood consumer.

One real proposal-research claim and one exact-revision reliance may be
associated with a proposal artifact. Proposal packets continue to own proposal
identity, current meaning, lifecycle, placement, uncertainty, relationships,
and decision authority. Candidate impact and refresh do not mutate the packet.

## References: implementation completion and Git history

Type: immutable source-event evidence; non-owning and non-authoritative for
claim semantics.

Existing commits, checkpoints, or completion receipts may provide real change
events and observed-path provenance. A dogfood impact nomination can reference
those facts through `sourced_by`. The source event cannot emit
`changed_because_of`, select a refresh outcome, or gain authority from being
durable.

## Defers: role state, scheduler, control plane, and compaction recovery

Type: prospective downstream integration; outside the first exercise.

Future roles may rely on exact claim revisions and future delivery machinery
may route refresh obligations. This proposal does not create those obligations,
wake roles, modify durable role state, or test context-discontinuity recovery.
The shared event identity may later be reusable, but reuse is an evidence need
rather than a current dependency.
