## Selection rule and outcome knowledge

The bounded population is the material reviewer-context claims in the adaptive
review candidate at `7959347` and the eight material lifecycle/evidence
findings in the initial `5367d9a` specialist review published at `7959347`.
Candidates had to satisfy all of these conditions:

1. the statement was real repository evidence rather than authored for this
   dogfood;
2. the statement and its evidence baseline were recoverable from immutable Git
   objects;
3. the accepted reviewer-session-retention completion at `b54d9b9` / public
   `fca5cbe6` plausibly nominated impact without itself needing semantic
   authority;
4. a later evidence world existed from which an authorized role could either
   retain or revise the conclusion; and
5. the identity question could remain stable while the evidence baseline
   changed.

The selector knew before selection that the research conclusion appeared to
hold and that the specialist finding was later reported closed. That knowledge
is why the selection is purposive. The expected outcomes below are planning
hypotheses, not terminal judgments.

### Exclusions

- The initial-review claims about minimum subject identity, lineage ownership,
  per-finding identity, synthesis integrity, empty outcomes, transition actors,
  and correction/invalidation were excluded because the reviewer-session
  implementation was not the clearest candidate-impact source for them.
- The initial-review context-lineage finding was selected over the adjacent
  fresh-entry claim because it has a later retained-specialist disposition that
  expressly addresses the same bounded question.
- Later synthesis-completeness and composition findings were excluded because
  their cleanest remediation events belong to the proposal-formation campaign,
  not the reviewer-session-retention completion used for the shared nomination
  exercise.
- Findings from the claim-lineage proposal's own review were excluded to avoid
  using the experiment's own durable review judgments as apparent independent
  evidence for its architecture.

No replacement fixture may be selected after dogfood encoding begins. A
blocked, ambiguous, or negative result remains the result of this selection.

## Shared semantic path

Both fixtures are material, attributed statements with a stable bounded
question, an immutable evidence baseline, a producer with limited authority, a
later accepted implementation completion that can only nominate impact, a
separate refresh episode and terminal judgment, and prospective reliance on an
exact resulting revision. They are not treated as equivalent domain objects:
the research fixture is a proposal-formation claim and the review fixture is a
specialist diagnostic finding.

One implementation completion may therefore nominate both exact revisions,
while each domain retains its own interpretation, refresh, reliance, and
reopening authority. For the changed review path, the nominating implementation
is not presumed to be the semantic cause.
