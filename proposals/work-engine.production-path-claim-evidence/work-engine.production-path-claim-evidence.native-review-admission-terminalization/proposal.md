# Establish native-review claim evidence through campaign terminalization

## Problem and desired outcome

PPCE-03 and PPCE-04 expose one selection-to-acceptance break: selection commands native-review path properties without carrying their evidence contract, while successful closure drops host-owned execution observations before Review Episode and Slice Campaign acceptance. A `reported` result can therefore cross an acceptance boundary without establishing configured independence, realization, capability, continuity, and subject-binding claims.

The desired permanent product change is a lossless chain from native-review selection through host execution observation, normalized result admission, Review Episode/native binding, terminal receipt, and campaign terminalization. A selected result may affect builder reliance or campaign acceptance only through an admission binding complete claim identity, admissible evidence, explicit status, and the authority route for false or unestablished outcomes.

## Exact bounded scope

In scope are selection-time required-claim declaration; host execution observation; backend-neutral admission; Review Episode and immutable candidate/native binding; terminal receipt and campaign consumption; correction, retry, recovery, and supersession; deterministic tests and migration audit.

Every required claim carries stable ID/revision, subject, covered state, consumption boundary, consumer, acceptance authority/source, claim-relative evidence profile, integrity-bound evidence, establishment status, and false/unestablished owner route.

Excluded:

- PPCE-01 remains a separate Review Bench identity correction; PPCE-02 remains a separate profile/path-provenance dependency.
- No universal independent observation.
- No provider call/replay, campaign launch, acceptance, roadmap change, implementation, cleanup, or raw source/intake mutation.
- No S14 ownership claim: idea-intake and proposal-former are pre-S14 verticals, not accepted S14 imports.
- The staging-path defect remains separate pre-S14 dogfood evidence and is not absorbed.

## Permanent contract versus transitional bootstrap

The proposal is the permanent normalized admission and terminalization contract, implemented across backends through evidence adapters and independent of Claude transcript formats.

Exact retained Claude session artifacts plus host execution records may be transitional evidence only under a separately accepted protocol naming exact artifacts, integrity/subject bindings, retention and sensitivity, covered claims, acceptance owner, expiry, and any provider/replay authority. No artifact set or protocol is supplied, so bootstrap evidence is not currently admissible and no provider call or replay is authorized.

## Invariants and ownership

DESIGN §3.9 controls:

- constraint prevents an invalid route but does not establish it;
- actor recording suffices only when the consumer accepts attributable conduct;
- independent observation applies only when the consumer requires outside attestation or the actor cannot know the history;
- missing admissible evidence is `unestablished`; contradictory admissible evidence is `false`; neither is inferred success;
- subject, covered state, boundary, and consumer are claim identity and cannot be silently narrowed;
- unestablished leaves the dependent condition unmet and routes to its owner without independently halting unrelated work.

Slice Supervisor owns selection and required-claim declaration, not truth. The reviewer host owns observations, not semantic acceptance. Normalized admission owns validation/profile evaluation and immutable admission identity, not waiver. Review Episode owns admitted-result lifecycle and exact bindings. Slice Campaign owns fail-closed consumption under its configured contract. Only the named acceptance authority may proceed without, narrow, replace, waive, or abandon the dependent condition. Providers, specialists, builders, adapters, and terminalizers cannot self-grant that authority.

## Evidence/admission model

Selection emits versioned `required_claims[]` with complete identity and a profile of permitted mechanisms, recorder/observer identities, relevant continuity/freshness conditions, integrity rules, and boundary status policy.

The host emits an immutable observation binding execution, candidate/subject, selection, requested and observed realization, capability/mutation envelope, session/continuity facts, transport digest, time, and observer. Adapters normalize without erasing native references.

Admission verifies freshness, all identity bindings, integrity, profile satisfaction, and result shape. It appends a normalized record binding result, evidence, per-claim `established`/`false`/`unestablished`, reasons, evaluator/version, and owner route. Review Episode binds admission and native result. Terminal receipts project sufficient identity, status, evidence locator/digest, and authority route. Campaign terminalization succeeds only when every consumed required claim is established or has a separate owner disposition; `reported` remains workload state.

Observation may be embedded or retained by stable integrity-bound reference, but must have one semantic owner, deterministic resolution, boundary-spanning retention, and explicit unavailable behavior.

## Correction/recovery

Malformed, mismatched, missing, expired, orphaned, or unverifiable evidence is rejected or preserved as `unestablished`; contradictory admissible evidence is `false`. Both route to the owner and are never overwritten. Repair/retry creates revisioned `corrects`/`supersedes` admissions. A changed subject or selection requires new identity. Recovery may re-resolve retained host evidence but never replay a provider or infer discarded evidence. Unavailable terminal evidence fails closed for that claim.
