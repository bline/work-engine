## Formation judgments

### Form one balancing-doctrine proposal

The semantic-durability candidate has one independently decidable consumer and
consequence: a model-facing judgment contract that makes context preservation
and retirement responsive to semantic value, risk, reconstructability, and
total lifecycle cost without prescribing a threshold or cadence.

The user clarification does not require a new raw-source revision because the
exact source already states the same consequence. It establishes that this
balance is central rather than incidental, which this proposal makes explicit.

### Keep empirical calibration deferred

The intake's empirical context-lifetime calibration candidate has a different
consumer, sensitivity, retention, evidence, and causal-limitation surface. It
requires stable observation, checkpoint, manifest, decision, transition, and
outcome identities that have not yet been exercised together. Forming it now
would add no current decision value and could prematurely select a research
owner or comparison contract. The candidate remains live in intake state for
later formation; it is not rejected or merged into this proposal.

### Do not duplicate bounded continuation mementos

The raw bounded-ad-hoc-continuation-state idea appears to refine role-owned
recovery rather than create another context-lifetime policy owner. This
proposal may recognize bounded mementos as one available representation when no
typed owner fits, but it does not form, own, or authorize that mechanism.

### Split mandatory preservation from economic preservation

Correctness-, authority-, obligation-, and effect-critical consequences may
require durable publication because losing them makes continuation invalid.
That causal boundary remains stronger than an economic preference.

Costly reconstruction alone does not establish the same invariant. Treating
all materially reconstructable meaning as mandatory would collapse the
proposal's situated cost judgment and could spend more tokens preserving state
than a retained context would cost. The formed proposal therefore treats
reconstruction materiality as evidence for judgment unless an owning role or
domain contract identifies a concrete protected consequence that makes durable
publication necessary.

### Probable doctrine and projection split

The general consequence fits governing Work Engine design because it defines
the relationship among context lifetime, semantic ownership, lifecycle cost,
and model judgment. Wind Walker is the probable runtime consumer and compact
projection owner because it governs safe context replacement. Exact placement
remains provisional until bounded use and instruction review establish what
must be loaded generally and what belongs only in a role-scoped runtime skill.
