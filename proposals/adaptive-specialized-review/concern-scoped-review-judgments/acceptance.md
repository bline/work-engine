# Acceptance: Concern-Scoped Review Judgments and Provenance

This file describes evidence that could support a later authorized evaluation.
It does not accept the proposal, authorize implementation, or change migration
priority.

## Semantic record cases

- A provider-neutral result binds exact candidate, perspective, reviewer
  configuration, episode generation, declared scope, evidence cutoff,
  limitations, and `complete_for_declared_scope` without implying acceptance.
- Positive, finding-bearing, incomplete, not-applicable, failed, unavailable,
  and uncertain results remain distinguishable.
- A no-finding judgment binds exact concern and subject revisions, relied-on
  evidence, exclusions, and limitations without claiming unlisted coverage.
- One concern-subject judgment can produce multiple findings without losing
  identity or remediation lineage.
- Corrections, supersession, composition, and successor judgments preserve
  immutable history.
- Episode retirement prevents continuation without deleting or invalidating
  published results.

## Mapping and normalization cases

- Explicit and declared-Cartesian grouped outputs materialize deterministic
  atomic judgment identities.
- Parallel concern and subject arrays without mapping semantics fail closed.
- Host normalization cannot invent a concern-subject pair, relied-on evidence,
  inspection assertion, disposition, or limitation.
- Grouped and atomic representations, if both admitted, produce equivalent
  query and lineage consequences without becoming duplicate judgments.

## Activity and cognition boundary cases

- Context presentation, retrieval, command execution, graph query, and browser
  capture remain observed activity rather than asserted inspection or reliance.
- A reviewer may assert inspection or reliance without a mechanically retained
  raw trace only with explicit evidence limitations.
- A raw event cannot manufacture `reviewed_for`, `no_material_issue_observed`,
  or finding validity.
- Redacted, omitted, unavailable, stale, and partial activity evidence remains
  visible.

## Delta and carry-forward cases

- Exact change evidence creates `possibly_affected` nominations rather than
  invalidation.
- A mechanically changed subject may receive a retained successor judgment when
  the change is attributed as irrelevant to the concern.
- An unchanged comparator result cannot carry a semantic judgment forward
  without an authorized relevance consequence.
- A retained successor binds predecessor judgment, successor candidate, delta,
  evidence, authority, rationale, cutoff, and limitations.
- Rename, move, split, merge, deletion, ambiguous matching, and unresolved
  endpoints preserve uncertainty.
- Concurrent and conflicting successor judgments remain visible.

## Ownership and authority cases

- Result publication does not retire the episode or accept the candidate.
- Episode state cannot rewrite published result or judgment history.
- Claim Evidence can retain a material finding reference without absorbing
  positive judgment, applicability, verdict, or acceptance authority.
- Code Change Profile can nominate impact without deciding semantic relevance.
- Codebase Memory can be rebuilt or unavailable without becoming a canonical
  record owner or losing review meaning.
- Missing provenance may expose uncertainty but cannot activate a reviewer.
- A human-authorized audit may intentionally establish missing review knowledge
  without weakening the preceding authority boundary.

## Observation and query cases

- Result-to-subject and subject-to-review navigation return exact owner,
  revision, per-source cutoffs, observed snapshot, coverage, limitations, and
  unresolved state.
- Canonical owner isolation: no source writes another owner's records. Review
  records are never written into a structural index, and observation or
  discovery cannot create, alter, or rebind a judgment or declared anchor.
- Exact-subject observation: an anchor observation that is not bound to the
  exact repository revision and one coherent, content-verified snapshot fails
  visibly as `unknown` and is not compared.
- Canonical publication: a result, its judgments, and their declared anchors
  publish atomically and retain unresolved endpoints. A composed query whose
  sources have inconsistent cutoffs is reported incomplete, never merged.
- Deterministic reconstruction: the derived anchor index and other query
  structures rebuild from canonical records. Their loss affects performance or
  freshness, never review meaning.
- A lost observation that cannot be reproduced exactly is represented as lost,
  not silently recomputed against different sources. Non-reproducible
  observations retained as evidence remain durable under their evidence
  owner's retention contract.
- A rename, move, deletion, or ambiguous split or merge of an anchored subject
  cannot produce "no affected judgments". Without explicit Code Change Profile
  correspondence, its anchors are reported unresolved or possibly affected with
  their cause. A path or qualified name is never treated as continuous
  identity.
- An empty reverse lookup is reported as "no affected judgments" only when
  source coverage, revision binding, and anchor-index completeness for the
  queried scope justify it. Otherwise it reports `unknown` or `unavailable`
  with its cause.
- Forward and reverse navigation stay bounded over realistic review history;
  reverse lookup from an exact subject or delta uses the anchor index rather
  than scanning history.
- `repo-search` returns provider-neutral evidence packets without exposing
  observation, indexing, or query-language procedure to reviewer contracts.

## Economic validation cases

- Counterfactual replay separates mechanically avoidable cost from uncertain
  alternative finding discovery.
- A bounded paired or shadow experiment uses immutable subjects, explicit truth
  ceilings, adjudication, budget, and stopping rules.
- Token and cost reductions are reported beside unique valid findings, missed
  findings, false findings, remediation rounds, and candidate churn.
- Provenance-output overhead is measured separately from semantic inference.
- The experimental comparator does not become a permanent production review
  obligation.

## Bootstrap evidence

- An accountable contract author produces the concern catalog, record contract,
  source-to-contract traceability, counterexamples, exclusions, and uncertainty.
- A separately defined independent implementation-contract review challenges
  semantic completeness, authority, evidence, and lifecycle meaning.
- The agent-instruction-review specialist reviews only materially normative
  model-facing instructions actually present in the candidate implementation.
- Deterministic regression fixtures preserve known protected distinctions,
  including causal exposure at the governed loading boundary.
- The new reviewer contract does not certify itself.
- An authorized human owner records any accepted contract change.
