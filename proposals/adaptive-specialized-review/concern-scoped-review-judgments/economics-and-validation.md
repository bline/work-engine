# Economics and Validation: Concern-Scoped Review

This document elaborates the economic objective and validation boundary of
[`proposal.md`](proposal.md). It does not set production routing policy,
reviewer admission, budget authority, or acceptance.

## Cost constitution

```text
store richly
query narrowly
infer only for an independently authorized judgment consequence
preserve prior meaning when relevance is established
measure whether cost falls without degrading adjudicated quality
```

Semantic richness in durable records must not imply placing the entire graph or
history into model context. Deterministic host machinery owns activity capture,
indexing, structural deltas, projection joins, comparator results, stable-ID
materialization, and mechanical validation. Model inference is reserved for
irreducible semantic judgment.

## Runtime cost boundaries

- A projection or provenance gap cannot authorize a review.
- Selected reviewers receive perspective- and concern-specific projections,
  evidence handles, and exact candidate deltas rather than a full graph dump.
- Shared evidence capture does not imply shared reviewer understanding.
- Reviewers assert relied-on evidence by reference rather than reproducing it.
- Compact judgment groups require explicit mapping semantics.
- Findings are synthesized before one bounded remediation when the owning
  workflow permits it.
- Retained reviewers receive relevant deltas and preserve the same episode when
  continuity remains trustworthy.
- Mechanically unchanged surfaces nominate carry-forward; they do not remove
  semantic review obligations by themselves.
- Full multi-perspective panels remain selected responses to consequence,
  novelty, uncertainty, or milestones rather than a universal default.

## Counterfactual replay

Historical replay estimates which costs a named alternative policy could have
avoided while preserving the exact observed trace and uncertainty about
alternative discovery quality. It separates:

- mechanically avoidable reconstruction and replacement;
- evidence retrieval that could have been shared or reused;
- concern-scoped deltas that a retained reviewer could have assessed;
- full reviews whose findings make omission counterfactuals uncertain; and
- costs that remain necessary under the alternative.

The replay binds the historical subject, evidence cutoff, policy revision,
pricing evidence, cache assumptions, retained invariants, omitted or substituted
events, uncertainty range, and states the historical trace cannot determine.
It cannot claim that a skipped review would have produced the same findings.

## Operational validation

A bounded experimental evaluation compares the proposed route with an existing
comprehensive route on immutable, adjudicable subjects. Useful measures include:

- provider cost, time, input, cache creation, cache reads, reasoning, and output;
- evidence-reconstruction and repository/browser exploration turns;
- fresh replacement and retained-delta frequency;
- unique material findings, duplicates, false findings, and false exoneration;
- findings missed by each route after adjudication;
- remediation rounds and candidate churn;
- cost per selected perspective, valid finding, verified resolution, candidate
  transition, and accepted slice; and
- provenance-output overhead separately from semantic reasoning cost.

The old comprehensive route may run as a paired or shadow comparator only for a
bounded S13 experiment with a budget, stopping rules, exact truth status, and
adjudication contract. It must not become a permanent production tax.

## Success and failure interpretation

Lower cost is not success if material adjudicated findings are lost. More
findings are not success if they are duplicates or false. A result is promising
when the route reduces reconstruction, replacement, repeated retrieval, and
candidate churn while preserving or improving adjudicated finding quality for
the declared concern and subject population.

The architecture has failed its economic objective if increasing durable
knowledge routinely increases context volume, reviewer invocation, or repeated
inference without a corresponding authorized consequence and adjudicated
quality benefit. That is an observed product failure, not automatic authority
to change routing or review contracts.
