# Relationships: Raw Idea Intake and Claim Assessment

## Enables: interactive proposal formation

Type: causal semantic handoff, not mandatory invocation order.

Intake supplies source-bound candidate meaning, assessed relationships,
evidence, uncertainty, authority, and reopening conditions. The proposal former
may challenge, split, merge, revise, or decline that meaning and creates
proposal identity only for candidates that survive formation. The formation
owner also publishes each complete candidate through the proposal-packet
contract at an immutable Git revision before semantic review; intake requires
that downstream outcome but does not perform or own publication.

## Related to: durable proposal packets

Type: provenance and downstream ownership relationship.

An intake record may become a stable origin for one or several proposal packets.
The packet retains ownership of proposal meaning, identity, lifecycle,
uncertainty, relationships, and decision authority. Intake readiness and packet
validation do not accept a proposal. Packet machinery supplies the mechanically
closed artifact that proposal formation publishes; it does not decide when a
candidate is semantically ready for publication or review.

## Migrates from: manual idea reconciliation

Type: attributed prior-evidence and compatibility-projection relationship.

`ideas/README.md` and `ideas/reconciliation-map.json` record the current
file-level reconciliation snapshot. Intake preserves those dispositions and
their evidence cutoff during migration, but re-adjudicates them at claim level.
After an exact source revision has an intake assessment, the manual snapshot is
historical provenance or a rebuildable coarser projection, not a competing
canonical disposition owner.

## Related to: claim-centered evidence lineage

Type: candidate shared-semantic relationship.

Stable assessment claims, evidence baselines, impact nominations, revisions,
and exact reliance may reuse the bounded claim-lineage minimum if later evidence
selects it as the shared owner. Intake can instead use a local equivalent while
that placement remains uncertain.

## Consumes: architecture, implementation, proposals, reviews, and decisions

Type: referenced evidence relationship.

Intake reconciles source claims against these stronger owners. It records its
evidence cutoff and attributed inference; it does not copy their canonical
truth or acquire their authority.

## Advises: repository cleanup

Type: non-authoritative consequence nomination.

An intake disposition may recommend movement, archival, redirect, merge, or
deletion. The repository mutation owner separately authorizes and records the
applied consequence while preserving stable provenance and references.
