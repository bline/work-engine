# Constrained retained-snapshot development qualification

Deliver one useful retained workflow on an explicitly controlled development
profile: a scoped snapshot read reaches its owner, survives context replacement
and restart, and supports a useful successor response without a second read or
invented authority. The [selected direction](../../../records/rust-lifecycle-s10-constrained-profile-direction-20261103.md)
follows a [real holder witness](../../../records/rust-lifecycle-s10-feasibility-result-20261103.md)
showing why a target trace alone cannot establish an outside holder's absence.

The proposed profile identifier is
`work-engine-retained-snapshot-development-v1`. This is a new qualification
claim, not a reinterpretation of the original native profile, its raw trace
status, eight completeness terms or 17 predicates. Its exact exclusions need
an owning acceptance decision before an implementation packet can rely on them.

## First delivery and its limits

Use fresh disposable task/lifecycle stores, a pinned packaged build and selected
backend/profile, and one preapproved immutable snapshot. The declared domain
surface is `workspace.snapshot_read`, ordinary response generation, and the
existing task-owner controls `task.question_open` and `task.result_publish`
where the selected retained workflow requires them. Those controls may create
only the admitted pending interaction or attributed result under existing
task/lifecycle authority; they grant no workspace mutation or approval. The
one-read count is distinct from these task-owner commits. Runtime bookkeeping
writes remain inside the named private roots and owners.
An offline scripted provider establishes mechanics first; a separately
authorized bounded live proof establishes actual semantic adequacy.

| Required outcome | Evidence owner and acceptance consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Exact scoped read and useful returned content | Existing operation owner binds task, delivery, attempt, grant, snapshot/member/range and committed bytes/digest; a marker absent from initial and later prompts reaches the successor from that result. Expected content is not observed content. |
| No duplicate owned read | Owner operation records and exact native/tool-loop history reconcile one read across replacement/restart; missing attribution or uncertain entry holds the task rather than authorizing another read. |
| Meaning survives two context replacements | Existing compiler/verifier and retained-task material preserve the completed consequence, governing constraints, superseded decision, unresolved approval and canonical references. Raw successor use is assessed, not just digest echo. |
| Pending input and authority remain correct | Canonical task/lifecycle owners retain question/recipient/scope identities, current grants and queued-input order. Data cannot grant mutation approval; stale or unavailable authority prevents dependent entry. |
| Restart does not create safe-retry fiction | Applicable pre/post-owner-commit cuts and current-store recovery preserve identity and uncertainty. A native terminal message or local process exit does not settle an unresolved effect. |
| Actual controlled composition | Launch/custody owner records executable/configuration/store identity, fresh starting state, effective capabilities and actual owned-process collection; unsupported or unestablished setup refuses admission. |

Historical import, semantic binary downgrade, broader tools, unrestricted
native execution, arbitrary backend switching and operational cutover are not
claims of this first delivery. The existing historical disposition, S9 and M6
owners retain their obligations. This profile cannot close full S10 merely by
passing its own narrower checks.

## Execution boundary and existing contracts

The candidate keeps the [owned-operation contract](../../../experiments/context-lifecycle-rust/DESIGN.md#owned-operations-rather-than-a-universal-tool-platform).
Exactly one admitted executor owns the tool loop. The selected harness must
actually exclude or enforce admission of undeclared model-invocable operations
before effects; an allowlist printed in a prompt or a wrapper around visible
calls is insufficient. Hidden direct-tool paths and unaccounted descendants
remain disqualifying. If the existing native route cannot establish that
boundary, report that route unsupported for this profile. A backend rewrite
does not become an automatic prerequisite or an implicit alternative.

The proposed environment has private fresh homes, stores and working roots;
read-only pinned tools and snapshot inputs; no ambient credentials; known
inherited channels; and one owned process scope with bounded lifetime and
actual collection. Network access is limited to the named scripted loopback
peer for offline work, or the separately approved provider route for live
proof. Actual setup and negative admission evidence must establish the claimed
boundary. Do not infer a closed resource world from a sandbox flag.

Evidence acquisition starts early enough for the specific claimed operation.
Known creation/ownership records, observation and enforcement may compose, but
each fact retains its producer, trust and limits. Where an inherited, shared,
external or remote resource is relevant to the operation's authority or
settlement, either establish that fact or refuse the affected entry. Omitting
the global-history claim never converts an unknown relevant effect into zero.

## Exact change in assurance claim

The original native profile keeps all its existing requirements and unresolved
standing. The candidate proposes the following separately identified evidence
boundary; it cannot turn an old `not_established` into `complete`.

| Original completeness term | Proposed development-profile treatment |
| --- | --- |
| `identity_and_once_signaling` | Preserve exact subject, operation, attempt, runtime and signal/collection identity where exercised; no numeric-identity fallback. |
| `bootstrap_history_frame_resource_publication` | Establish the selected launch/initial-resource and publication boundary through named owners and actual setup evidence; no claim of exhaustive kernel bootstrap history. |
| `original_independent_kernel_world_target_history` | No complete independent kernel/world-history claim. This is an explicit assurance reduction requiring acceptance; relevant facts needed for admission/settlement remain required. |
| `target_history_and_effect_closures` | Preserve exact domain/native tool-loop history and settlement of every admitted operation and owned descendant; no arbitrary-syscall completeness claim. |
| `resources_denial_lifetimes_cardinalities` | Establish scope, denial, identity, lifetime and count facts determining the selected operation; never infer arbitrary external-holder absence from target exit. |
| `mm_permits_all_resume_cancellation` | Preserve applicable runtime authority, cancellation and currentness guarantees; no new claim covering every kernel memory/resume transition. |
| `graceful_tasks_echild_collector_bounds` | Preserve actual owned-process waits/collection and deadlines required by the chosen scope; no universal host-process settlement claim. |
| `effects_caller_store_host_custody` | Preserve attributable caller, selected build/configuration, store, grant and executed-operation custody needed by the profile. No assertion of exhaustive unrelated OS history. |

For the 17 original predicates, exact selection/store/task/effect/attempt/
delivery/host identity, approved peer, applicable resource lifetimes, controlled
exit and budget remain necessary consequences. The new profile does not assert
the original `v2_history_resources_permits`, `observer_atomic_bootstrap`,
`observer_bootstrap_fence`, `single_tracer_effects` or `exec_objects` predicates
by substituting a smaller test. The implementation packet must map every
original predicate and applicable T01–T37 case to preserved evidence, a proposed
claim change or an honestly unresolved dependency. Reuse accepted evidence only
where its subject and meaning apply. Do not edit the old predicate semantics.

## Finite route to qualification

The first decision is the exact claim change and whether one existing route can
enforce the declared operation surface and supply its necessary evidence. A
profile-level gap is returned as an unsupported route or explicit alternative,
not expanded into an unbounded prerequisite project.

Once that boundary is accepted, a single bounded implementation packet names
the smallest remaining composition delta, complete review and validation, then
the exact controlled qualification and separately authorized live proof. Its
total forecast includes preparation, all actors, recovery, review and closure.
No implementation cost/date is established by this proposal; no old S10-F
allowance is available for it. New failures may justify in-scope repair, but
changed claims or infeasible forecasts return to the decision owner.

This proposal contains normative candidate admission and acceptance text for
future agents, principally in “Execution boundary and existing contracts” and
“Exact change in assurance claim.” It introduces no instruction-loading rule
or mandatory observation mechanism. Review those exact candidate consequences
before using the profile as an execution contract.
