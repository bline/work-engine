## What the manual route taught

A, B, C1, and C2 show that a coordinator can dispatch one current bounded packet, collect a worker result, have a supervisor check the exact subject and retained review, then refresh dependent evidence before the next dispatch. B's split arose from independently verifiable outcomes, not a fixed size. The later C2 repair is the strongest negative example: a passing, well-bound packet result was still the wrong schema subject. D shows that private readiness can be reported precisely while integration and product acceptance remain with their owners. A packet's listed obligations were prose; neither tight wording nor many hashes demonstrates exhaustive decomposition.

The useful artifact distinctions are demonstrated by the [C2 packet](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/slices/C2/packet-v1.md), [v1 worker handoff](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/slices/C2/handoff-v1.md), [v3 result](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/slices/C2/result-v3.json), [supervisor acceptance](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/slices/C2/acceptance-v1.json), and [B refresh](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/refresh-v10.json). A worker's completion or unfinished handoff never decided acceptance. B's refresh changed the current-blocking readback basis and explicitly invalidated old bindings; simply changing the hash beside the old claim would have been false. C2's v1/v2 observations remain historical after v3 fixed the subject. The C1 setup miss, C2 schema miss, and D before/after limit argue for binding the actual host, store schema, source and result before relying on a claim, and for recording semantic invalidation as well as new bytes.

The retained Astra episode was recovered by an authorized generation-2 successor with `reconstructed_continuation`; it was not a new independent review. C1/C2/D reports are immutable external evidence, but their review-state CAS linkage is still absent. The current adapter would overwrite that continuity with `same_session`. The bounded acceptance owners did not require a per-slice CAS as their semantic acceptance condition; the review-state infrastructure owner still owes a truthful repair and linkage. A valid review report, its durable state linkage, the slice acceptance decision, and root integration are separate claims.

The experiment report can now close as a **bounded method evaluation**. The product campaign remains open: original two P2 findings; live compiler/verifier/successor adequacy; G2 paired-store rollback; human M6; historical import and related profile/retirement questions; TO2 authority and three unqualified tests; review-state continuity-preserving linkage; and root's exact candidate integration decision. No paid/live trial, migration, activation, commit, push or main integration was performed in this experiment. A separately scoped infrastructure repair can address the CAS adapter; it should not be smuggled into C1/C2/D product acceptance. Root should assess each remaining product obligation under its owning accepted contract and actual gate requirements, without turning every open item into an invented D gate.

## Measurement and cross-effort limits

The run had no matched monolithic baseline and its recoverable rollout counters were not collected, so whether it reduced avoidable reconstruction, total inference, repair effort or escaped defects is unknown. The observed setup and subject repairs are costs, but the records do not support a complete comparable cost denominator. There is no sealed-key score for decomposition omissions. Packet-obligation accounting appeared in prose and examples, not an exhaustive, independently adjudicated obligation ledger; no coverage score is manufactured. A future comparison needs the same known work, model/effort, acceptance conditions, and complete coordinator/packet/refresh/review costs, with held-out omission checks if a decomposition-quality claim is sought. Prospective enrollment and controls for the next campaign must be decided **before** its coordinator launches. D launched before such designation and cannot be relabeled prospective.
