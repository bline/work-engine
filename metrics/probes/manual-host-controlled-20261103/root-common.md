# Common episode-root packet

This packet becomes executable only with the study owner's exact registration,
environment and budget approval, recorded qualification and your allocated arm.
Before that it is a prospective packet, not a launch grant.

You own one isolated episode of the task in `tasks.json`. Read the approved
preregistration and your task plus the frozen repository DESIGN/AGENTS and the
manual-orchestration supervisor references named by the skill. Load them only
from the materialized environment. Your semantic decisions remain yours:
decomposition within the task, child packets, judgment, acceptance, abandonment
and final report. The experiment controller owns collection and study accounting;
it is not an engineering coordinator providing unmeasured bookkeeping.

The treatment is the separately supplied H or M condition. The common contract
requires observed exact-turn model/effort/tier and native grant evidence,
verified runtime identity and collection ownership, context and budget bindings,
artifact/result/custody integrity, review, truthful failure states and explicit
closure. Skipping one of these changes the comparison rather than improving
efficiency. Required information may use the arm's natural record formats.
Paths and identity substitutions come from your bound run record; a role name
or inherited environment SID is not a verified identity.

No production work is assigned. Mutate only your experimental worktree and
run-local records, preserve others' work and use the experiment's isolated
coordination board. Do not read other arms, other roots' private records,
later source or sealed benchmarks, change global configuration, publish code,
deploy, access external product services or use credentials. The realized
allowlist is part of the input manifest; instructions alone do not establish
isolation. Stop admission and preserve evidence if a required grant or bound
input is unavailable. Do not manufacture a broader grant from filesystem policy.

Requested root is Sol6.1/xhigh/default; workers Sol6.1/medium/default and fresh
reviewers Astra/high/default, with exact IDs from the protocol. No fallback or
extra role. Give every actor a bounded packet and record actual settings. You
are not alone in the filesystem: do not revert another actor's edits. Retain
your P05 worker for its second specified stage after checking capacity; use a
fresh reviewer, since retained read-only realization is not established by this
host version. Do not invent remediation findings to force the planned extension.

Your episode cap is $10 across actors. Your own cap is $5: stop substantive work
at $3.50 and preserve $1.50 for reporting and collection. Workers together have
$2, reviewers together $2.50 and the remaining $0.50 is unallocated headroom.
Bootstrap, failed launches and repairs count. The registered productive boundary
is 30 minutes and hard return is 45 minutes from your initial entry. An allowance
is a maximum, not a target. Reconcile spending at meaningful boundaries using the
canonical reader; don't request a new model turn just to poll. No budget/clock
reset, silent model change, outside helper or unbounded retry. Tell the collector
when a required outcome remains unestablished, while preserving live custody.

Write bounded task packets and use ordinary capable role behavior. The public
tests describe examples, not permission to hardcode their answers. Source
reviewers return evidence-backed findings or an explicit no-finding verdict,
with inspected scope and limitations. Reviewers receive source and contract,
not your condition label or narrative. Record unavoidable unblinding.

Judge the result against the full public contract. Never turn a missing report,
settings mismatch, unverified artifact or unfinished process into acceptance.
Capture final artifacts and exact hashes, review verdict/repairs, actual actor
IDs and original assignment, close/acknowledge the episode's records and return
one compact final report with outcome, evidence paths, unknowns and collection
responsibility. Your final report and closure are part of the measured work.
