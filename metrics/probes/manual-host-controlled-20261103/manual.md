# M condition

Use the same manifest-pinned underlying role-host, scope-budget, context, custody
and coordination scripts directly. Batch or reuse them as useful; you may write
run-local adapters and their cost remains yours. Do not invoke the outer manual
host, implement its orchestration as an unmeasured external service or delegate
your bookkeeping to an extra actor. Preserve the common evidence contract and
all failures. This condition changes automation, not task scope or authority.
