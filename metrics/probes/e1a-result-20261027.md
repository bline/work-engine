# E1a result: run-to-run variation of first artifacts on usage-export (2026-10-27)

Pre-registration: [`e1a-prereg.md`](e1a-prereg.md). Environment manifest:
[`e1a-environment.json`](e1a-environment.json) (`71d7ad2b…`). Scored by F3.1
(`--interface e1a-v1`, Terra final-tier rules with 3 pre-run adaptations). The
coordinator read only the scorer's counts-only output.

**Result class: pilot estimate obtained.** All 5 arms produced a committed,
interface-conformant artifact, and all 5 are scoreable. n = 3 Sonnet runs, and
1 run each for Haiku and Opus.

**Post-hoc qualification (2026-10-27): most of the headline measure is not
contract compliance.** An independent check after the runs found:

- Only 12 of the 34 battery cases trace to a clause in the E1a packet.
- 58 of the 62 confirmed failures across the five arms fall on cases that either
  over-constrain or rest on requirements the packet does not state.
- On the traced cases, Sonnet failed 2, 0 and 0 (range 2).

The pre-registered reading below stands as registered. See
[Battery traceability](#battery-traceability-post-hoc-not-pre-registered).

  - H1, 14 / 7: inside the Sonnet range. It fails no case outside the Sonnet union.
  - O1, 9 / 5: 2 failures below the Sonnet minimum. It fails 1 case that no Sonnet
    run failed.
- **Across all five arms:** 17 distinct failing cases, 5 common to all; 11
  families, 2 common to all.
- **By fixture-provenance group (S1):** most failures fall in the group of 26
  fixtures that the historical final reviewer added (11 failures). The categories
  are `not established`, as in F3.
- **Pre-registered E1b consequence: triggered.** The Sonnet range in confirmed
  failures is 4, which is 3 or more. Single E1b runs are therefore treated as
  unable to separate models on confirmed-failure differences smaller than 4, and
  the E1b replication trigger applies to them.

## Observations not pre-registered

Descriptive only; each is n = 3 + 1 + 1.

- **Own checks and the battery.** Every arm passed its own tests, `fmt` and
  clippy.
  - Full battery: 9–15 failures.
  - The 12 cases that trace to the packet: 0–2 failures, and 0 for 3 of the
    5 arms.
  - **Correction (2026-10-27).** This bullet first said that a builder's green
    checks did not indicate first-artifact quality on this task. That read battery
    failures as defects without checking that the cases trace to the packet. The
    post-hoc split shows most failures fall on cases the packet does not support.
- **First artifacts were cheap and fast compared with the historical build
  component** (Sonnet $4.02, Opus $7.19 for design, implementation and repairs
  under review). That figure is not comparable: it includes design review and
  repair loops, and a different interface.

  Those followed an Astra design review and used each builder's own schema.

**Reading:**
- **Traced cases, Sonnet.** Failures were 2, 0 and 0, a range of 2. 2 distinct
  cases failed, both in S1 only; none failed in all three runs.
- **Where the full-battery range comes from.** Each Sonnet run failed 5 cases
  that rest on requirements outside the packet. Whether those are the same
  5 cases was not computed. The full-battery range of 4 therefore comes from
  the over-constraining cases (range 2) and the traced cases (range 2).
- **What the headline measures.** It mostly measures agreement with
  review-derived and historical choices, not compliance with the packet.
- **The pre-registered E1b consequence stands as registered.** That is the
  range of 4 and the replication trigger.
  - On traced cases the range is 2, which would not have triggered it. But that
    reading is post hoc, rests on one checker, and covers 12 cases with n = 3, so
    it does not replace the registered reading.
  - E1b uses T0 batteries, which are checked for traceability before use; spread
    should be re-estimated on them.
- **Limits of the classification:**
  - One case moves from traced to over-constraining under a lenient reading of
    "invalid counter"; no arm failed it.
  - The split between over-constraining and not-in-packet is a tie-break
    judgement. The traced set does not depend on it.
  - Three traced cases rely on behaviour of the existing reader rather than on
    packet text.
  - The checker's report contains one sentence inconsistent with its own table
    ("4 of the 5 arms" where the table shows 3). The recomputed table is the one
    used here.

## Limitations

- **n.** n = 3 for the spread; one task; one interface.
- **Network** was not confined inside the sandbox.
- **Prior model exposure** to this code cannot be excluded.
- **Shape hints in the interface.** It is Terra's reviewed structure, so it carries
  some review-derived shape. Its fixed reason codes are limited to conditions the
  contract names.
- **The scorer's 3 adapted checks** are a one-time judgment made before the runs
  by the F3.1 builder. They are recorded in sealed notes.
- **Battery traceability** was checked only after the runs. See
  [Battery traceability](#battery-traceability-post-hoc-not-pre-registered).
  22 of the 34 cases do not trace to the packet.
- **Commits as submitted.** Arm commits are scored as the builders left them; no
  arm left uncommitted changes.
