# Requirement provenance analysis: DOC-4 to DOC-9

**Parent identification (my reading).**
- DOC-3 names its bases as "RL `041-u4-protection-qualification-PLAN.md` and `043-u4a-held-contract.md`". DOC-2's text ("Contract and open implementation checkpoint: `043-u4a-held-contract.md`") reads as 041. DOC-1's text matches what DOC-5 attributes to "RL `043`".
- The derived documents cite "`review-r2/PLAN.md`". Its content matches DOC-3's H1–H5. However, DOC-3 says it "has not been re-reviewed", while the cited plan "had cleared **as a static plan**". I therefore treat DOC-3 as the closest parent text, but I cannot confirm it is the identical artefact.
- Several cited sources are not among the parents, so I cannot use them as support. These include `QUALIFICATION-AMENDMENT.md`, `docs/contracts/...`, the board items and the Astra reports.

**Parent authority statements used throughout:**
- DOC-2: "RL owns task-host and lifecycle admission/receipt semantics; dev-vertical owns the actual Pi Host controller and rendered container boundary."
- DOC-3: "The latter documents [RL 041/043] own the broader qualification contract."
- DOC-3: "Dev-vertical owns coordination, result collection and the separate acceptance decision."
- DOC-3: "coordinate any actual shared protocol or pin change with RL."

## DOC-4 (escalation request)

### Requirements and how they are presented

1. **Scope limit.** "It is not authority for broader design, real Docker/preflight/H4, live/installed changes, RL R1, commit or integration." / "Host and witness remain frozen, read-only."
   - Presented as: a restriction under a new operator grant ("overnight continuation") that is not in the parents.
   - Parent: it only narrows what is permitted. It is consistent with DOC-2 ("No startup release, live inference, paid run … is authorized") and DOC-3 ("No live calls, credentials …").
   - Not flagged.

2. **Preservation.** "Preserve all named prior findings and passed controls."
   - Presented as: inherited from documents outside the parents.
   - Not a weakening. Not flagged.

3. **Open question about causal removal.** The counterexample is "successful exact-ID absence can then yield `held_cancelled` without affirmative Host removal". The doc then asks: "Test whether intended success genuinely requires causal Host removal under H1–H5/RL, rather than infer that from absence".
   - Presented as: proposed. The question of actor attribution is explicitly left for the reviewer.
   - Parent support is only a reading:
     - DOC-1: "The Host owns the child, same-session framed handshake, and exact stop."
     - DOC-1: "concludes only after … exact container removal observed against the owned identity."
   - Not flagged, because the doc does not impose the requirement.

4. **Five findings to account for.** "early all-available initialize-pin check, canonical/actual ready bytes vs repeated digest, actual subprocess EOF/timeout custody, connection framing and finality on the same identity stream, and digest-bound private helper evidence".
   - Presented as: review findings put to the reviewer, not imposed.
   - Partial parent support:
     - DOC-3 H2: "Reject wrong pin/profile/session".
     - DOC-3: "require the exact ready digest for every `held_cancelled`".
     - DOC-3: "an empty name lookup or arbitrary inspect failure cannot confirm absence".
   - Not flagged.

5. **Constraints on the review.** "keep a single Host verdict and fixture read-only, with no new consumer projection unless RL requires it"; "do not invent active-run or mutation authority"; "do not waive compiled validation or later H4 approval"; "without promising durability after all storage fails or exposing raw inspect data".
   - Parent: consistent with DOC-3 ("RL's consumer check (R1) … read-only") and DOC-1 ("do not serialize raw environment").
   - Not flagged.

## DOC-5 (architectural review)

The doc presents its design as "a recommendation for dev-vertical's disposition". It also asserts binding readings of the existing contract.

### D5-1. Host-attributed normal removal

**Text:** "The Host must establish that it completed the required cleanup action, not merely that every process in its log was clean." / "For this realization, **yes, an affirmative Host-attributed normal removal is required.**"

**Presented as:** inherited, by reading the contract.

**Properties demanded:**
- The Host brings about the outcome.
- Actor attribution evidence is required.
- A normal (not forced) action is required.

**What the parents plainly state:**
- DOC-1: "The Host owns the child, same-session framed handshake, and exact stop."
- DOC-1: "concludes only after bounded receiver/attach exit collection and exact container removal observed against the owned identity."
- DOC-3 H4: "successful bounded receiver/attach exits without fallback stop/kill → successful independent post-exit query confirming the original full container ID absent".

**Reading:** "owns … exact stop" can be read to mean the Host performs the removal. However, "removal observed" states observation, not attribution. "without fallback stop/kill" qualifies the receiver/attach exits, not container removal.

**Cited support check:** "RL `041:13–17` already distinguishes physical cleanup from clean held cancellation in the earlier EOF probe".
- DOC-2 says the probe "establishes candidate readiness and physical cleanup, **not** clean held cancellation". The reason is that "Controller EOF collected and removed the exact container", i.e. there was no accepted cancel.
- The cited text does not say who must perform removal.

**Result:** flag. Actor attribution goes beyond the plain text, and the cited support does not state it.

### D5-2. Affirmative result under actual semantics

**Text:** "require an attributable normal operation against the original full ID and launch Engine, with an affirmative result under that operation's actual semantics… If a purported acknowledgement could equally mean "already missing," it cannot by itself establish this distinction." Also, for a clean verdict: "no fallback intervention". The test plan adds "forced recovery that removes the right ID but cannot qualify".

**Parent:** DOC-3 plainly requires "Distinguish confirmed removal, removal failure and unknown cleanup". There is no parent text requiring that a normal removal be distinguished from "already absent", or that a forced removal of the right ID disqualifies.

**Result:** flag (evidence property beyond the parents).

**Not flagged:** "Narrowing success to eventual absence regardless of how it happened would require an owner decision". This preserves rather than weakens.

### D5-3. Total private action result (P0 table, "Recommended")

**Presented as:** the author's design choice.

**Parent:** within DOC-3's "Distinguish confirmed removal, removal failure and unknown cleanup" and "If create is uncertain and trusted identity cannot be recovered, report cleanup unknown".

**Plainly supported:** "A create that may have reached the Engine without attributable identity is **not** proof of non-creation."

**Result:** not flagged.

### D5-4. Safe recovery coordinate

**Text:** "preserve a **safe recovery coordinate**—run/job, bound Engine, original ID and its attribution—until a replacement is known persisted… If the owner write fails … do not qualify."

**Presented as:** "minimum correction", i.e. required.

**Parent:**
- DOC-1: "if process/container state is unavailable, retain a cleanup-unknown result and any known diagnostics".
- DOC-3: "Preserve failure evidence and cleanup disposition even without readiness."

The persistence-ordering property ("until a replacement is known persisted") is not stated.

**Plainly supported:** "Do not retain or expose raw inspect output" (DOC-1: "do not serialize raw environment"; DOC-3: "Do not retain raw Docker environment").

**Result:** flag (beyond the parents).

### D5-5. Joined acceptance publication boundary

**Text:** "require the actually received matching reply, matching persisted terminal and bound private evidence, observed successful exit of that exact child, then the independent exact-ID result and retained run manifest."

**Parent:**
- DOC-3 H4 orders the "post-exit query" after "receiver/attach exits".
- DOC-3 requires "result digests, sanitized log/receipt paths".
- No parent requires the fixture to observe the Host child's exit, or the received reply, before the query.

**Result:** flag (ordering and evidence beyond the parents).

### D5-6. Initialize pins before start

**Text:** "Before sending start, compare **all independently available expected pins** in initialize".

**Parent:**
- DOC-3 H2: "Reject wrong pin/profile/session".
- DOC-3 H5: "Mismatched … job/pin … never yield a valid held-ready record".
- DOC-2 trace: "`pi-host/initialize` reports identity before container startup".
- No parent orders the pin comparison before start.

**Result:** flag (ordering).

### D5-7. Ready digest before cancel

**Text:** "Verify the digest of the **actual ready value under the exact Host encoding**, before cancel and in the retained join."

**Parent:**
- DOC-3 H4: "explicit cancel bound to that record".
- DOC-3: "require the exact ready digest for every `held_cancelled`".

**Reading:** a reasonable reading of "bound to that record". "a new shared serialization protocol is not required" is consistent with DOC-1 ("No change to ordinary serve or shared activation protocol").

**Result:** not flagged.

### D5-8. Child custody

**Text:** "request EOF first on failure… If EOF fails, either a **pre-agreed, capable process custodian** collects the exact child, or an explicitly authorized policy permits it to terminate and reap that child… With no such route, do not start a real fixture."

**Parent:**
- Plainly supported: "EOF is not accepted cancel" (DOC-3: "EOF/failure closure cannot substitute for the accepted cancel").
- DOC-2: "Verify no leftover child/container, or retain a named cleanup-unknown outcome."
- No parent gives an EOF-first ordering or a custody precondition on starting a real fixture.

**Result:** flag (ordering and a new gate).

### D5-9. Response finality

**Text:** "Require one complete, unambiguous response to each request on the identified stream… If the actual permitted Engine cannot supply this finality or an equivalent binding, independent absence remains unestablished."

**Parent:**
- DOC-3: "an empty name lookup or arbitrary inspect failure cannot confirm absence".
- DOC-3: "retain how effective daemon selection was bound across commands".
- Same-stream finality is not stated.

**Result:** flag. The connection-close mechanism itself is offered as a "viable design" and is not flagged.

### D5-10. Private sidecar sealing

**Text:** "read/hash the same bounded private bytes… seal their digest…"; "RL decides whether this projection serves its consumer."

**Parent:** DOC-3: "Bind the witness to …", "result digests", and "agree which ready and terminal fields the consumer needs".

**Result:** treated as the author's way of meeting binding. Not flagged.

### D5-11. Authority

**Text:** "Dev-vertical owns the local Host amendment and offline disposition. RL owns any change in clean-close or consumer meaning".

**Analysis:** D5-1 and D5-2 add new conditions for `held_cancelled`, which is the clean-close outcome. DOC-5 nevertheless classes them as a "local amendment to the qualification contract" owned by dev-vertical. By DOC-3 the qualification contract is RL-owned ("The latter documents own the broader qualification contract").

**Result:** flag (authority).

### D5-12. H4 approval gate

**Text:** "The fixture-authorizing owner must separately approve an exact real H4 run, including timeout custody, **before** preflight or launch."

**Parent:** no parent names a "fixture-authorizing owner" or this gate. The closest is DOC-3: "Before dispatch, confirm implementation authority".

**Result:** flag (new gate and owner).

### D5-13. Compiled gate

**Text:** "The compiled gate remains owed: focused controls actually selected, complete proof and ordinary libraries, both selector behaviors, and extracted receiver comparison… linked-table sentinel".

**Parent:** selector behaviour is supported by DOC-3 ("test ordinary launch **without** the selector… Keep non-proof selector refusal separate"). The rest cites sources outside the parents.

**Result:** flag (cited support not in the parents).

### D5-14. Plainly supported items (not flagged)

- "Keep the corrected distinction between a trusted create ID… and unknown ownership" (DOC-3 create-uncertain rule).
- "A failed verdict can truthfully carry `confirmed_absent`".
- "Do not … remove a container from the observer" (DOC-3: RL consumer check "read-only").
