### Candidate 2: Context-replacement outcome evidence

The source separately proposes recording attributed replacement decisions and
later outcomes so future research can compare retention, intentional
replacement, and worker replacement. That consequence has a different

This candidate includes reset-event identity, decision evidence references,
context-window lineage, selected route, observed rehydration work, token and
latency consequences, correctness and review outcomes, and explicit missing
measurements. It excludes policy thresholds, causal claims inferred from one
episode, raw-trace ownership, and operational authority.

The raw-agent-execution evidence archive is a plausible evidence source, not
the semantic owner of compact measurements or research conclusions. Whether
this candidate should form immediately or remain downstream of the live
observability candidate is an explicit proposal-formation decision.
