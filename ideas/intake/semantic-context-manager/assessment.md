# Semantic Context Manager Intake Assessment

## Subject

This assessment binds the complete revised `Semantic Context Manager` raw idea
at its immutable source checkpoint. The source retains its own wording and
nuance; this narrative records one attributed intake interpretation.

## Assessment

The source contains one claim whose independent identity has downstream
decision value:

> Allocate periodic context-lifetime monitoring, semantic-difference
> inspection, bounded active-agent validation, preservation coordination,
> rehydration verification, and retirement judgment to a host-orchestrated
> specialist for non-human-facing agents, using provider-neutral runtime
> observations and an exact two-phase handoff.

This is not merely a restatement of semantic-durability doctrine. The formed
semantic-durability proposal owns what safe and economically grounded context
retirement means. This source contributes a candidate allocation of runtime
responsibilities and an experiment shape intended to remove continuous
self-management from ordinary agents.

The hooks-to-app-server design does not warrant a second candidate. Provider
actor observation and capability reporting already have a plausible owner in
the runtime-adapter idea, while host capability discovery belongs to the
environment adapter. For this intake, those mechanisms are dependencies and
relationship nominations inside the Context Manager candidate.

## Disposition

The claim is ready for proposal formation. That disposition means only that a
fresh proposal former may decide whether and how to form the bounded candidate.
It does not accept the idea, approve implementation, establish permanent
placement, or change roadmap priority.

The candidate boundary includes:

- one host-orchestrated specialist role for non-human-facing agents;
- cheap host-owned lifecycle monitoring and actor discovery;
- a provider-neutral runtime-observation projection;
- hooks as the first limited Codex realization and app-server events as the
  richer target realization;
- immutable snapshot identity and a stale-packet retirement fence;
- bounded active-agent omission or distortion checks;
- owner-validated preservation writes;
- two-phase rehydration with old-context recoverability until acknowledgement;
- shadow-mode evaluation before effectful replacement; and
- total lifecycle cost and continuation-correctness evidence.

It excludes:

- replacement of semantic-durability, Wind Walker, role-state, authority, or
  domain owners;
- a full runtime or environment adapter implementation;
- automatic migration between an active CLI actor and app-server thread;
- fixed inspection thresholds or automatic reset policy;
- a claim that transcript or fork APIs expose the exact effective model input;
- generalized human-facing context replacement;
- proposal acceptance, implementation, cleanup, or roadmap authority.

## Material uncertainty

- The runtime does not yet establish a trusted exact effective-model-input
  snapshot containing every applicable instruction, skill, message, tool
  result, authority event, and revision identity.
- Hooks may support discovery, inspection signals, and compaction blocking but
  not live occupancy, actor enumeration, exact turn forks, or controlled
  replacement.
- The host may be unable to keep the old context recoverable while a fresh
  context rehydrates and proves the handoff.
- Specialist inspection may consume more tokens and attention than it saves.
- Active-agent validation may either miss omissions or recreate the continuous
  context-management burden the design seeks to remove.
- The appropriate canonical placement among role, runtime, environment, and
  governing design projections remains unresolved.
- Transport trust classification, confidentiality, retention, and
  prompt-injection boundaries require stronger review before effectful use.

## Handoff

A proposal former should receive one candidate and the named evidence in the
projection. It should preserve the stronger semantic-durability, role-state,
authority, and runtime ownership boundaries; it may challenge, split, merge, or
decline the intake interpretation.

The first proposed implementation campaign, if separately authorized later,
should test one non-human-facing builder in shadow mode. It should not perform
effectful replacement until snapshot and two-phase handoff capabilities are
demonstrably reachable.
