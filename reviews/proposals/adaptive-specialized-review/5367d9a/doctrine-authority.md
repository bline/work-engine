# Doctrine and Authority Review

Role: read-only doctrine-and-authority reviewer. Consequence: diagnostic and
advisory; no proposal or implementation authority.

Subject: the exact revision and content set in [subject.md](subject.md).

## Findings

1. **Material, observed — the canonical proposal does not preserve the
   doctrine/philosophy authority distinction.** The coordination proposal names
   doctrine review and `contract_conflict`, but does not say that a blocking
   conflict requires lineage to binding `DESIGN.md`, an accepted contract, or
   an authority boundary. A fresh packet consumer could elevate a
   philosophy-only tension into a blocking conflict. Canonical proposal meaning
   must make philosophy-only alignment findings advisory and nonblocking.
2. **Material, observed — applicability judgment has no named semantic decision
   owner.** The artifact contract may define and record applicability,
   partial-applicability, refresh, and supersession, but neither proposal says
   who may make those judgments. Persistence cannot implicitly acquire that
   authority. The proposal must name the role or preserve the choice as an
   unresolved authority need before contract acceptance.
3. **Moderate, observed — the applicability command is broader than its causal
   parent.** Requiring a new applicability judgment for every later reuse also
   captures reuse against an unchanged immutable subject. Narrow the command to
   changed subject/evidence or consequential reuse for which applicability is
   not already established.

## Supported conclusions

- Persistence is consistently separated from correctness, independence,
  acceptance, and authority; no circular self-validation was found.
- Specialist selection and omission remain consequence-driven judgment rather
  than a fixed roster.
- Freshness is scoped to the independence claim and actual supplied context;
  the proposal does not improperly require a new process for every continuation.
- Synthesis preserves disagreement and remains advisory.
- Decision readiness remains distinct from acceptance, roadmap mutation, scope
  change, and implementation authorization.
- The inter-proposal dependency is causal only for the accepted durable-output
  consequence; it does not establish delivery order.

## Conflicts and uncertainty

- “Artifact contract owns applicability semantics” could mean vocabulary
  ownership or authority to judge. Canonical text must disambiguate it.
- A single selection/synthesis context and a future fixed initial reviewer set
  pose nonblocking philosophical tensions already exposed as uncertainties.
- Proposal-local versus shared placement and the material-revision threshold
  remain legitimately unresolved.

## Limitations and applicability

This review does not decide value, priority, final placement, or implementation
feasibility. It applies only to the bound subject and must be reconsidered when
canonical proposal meaning or governing doctrine changes.

Readiness consequence: `revision_warranted`.
