   references to every finding or conflict the synthesis *consumes*. Neither
   requires a disposition for every material finding/conflict produced by the

   Mechanical closure requires a complete episode-output inventory with an

## Mechanical versus semantic boundary

Mechanically checkable:

- stable subject, statement, episode, finding, and artifact identities;
- exact revision/content-set digests and evidence references;
- closed review outcomes and required reasons;
- provenance and authority-reference presence;
- lineage relation vocabulary, target resolution, and non-destructive history;
- context-continuation references;
- synthesis references, conflict references, and coordinator-inference
  attribution; and
- if added, complete disposition coverage over the episode's material output
  inventory.

Necessarily semantic:

- whether a statement is material or supported;
- whether assumptions and sensitivity surfaces are sufficient;
- whether freshness or decision-specific applicability is correct;
- whether a reviewer was meaningfully independent;
- whether omission, severity, confidence, or composition is justified;
- whether synthesis is intellectually faithful; and
- whether residual uncertainty is acceptable for a downstream decision.
