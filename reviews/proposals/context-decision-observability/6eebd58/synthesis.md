# Initial Review Synthesis

The panel supports revision rather than rejection. The candidate remains an
independently useful live benefit projection, but the initial text assigns too
much semantic weight to packet composition and too little to the owners of
authority, lifecycle transitions, evidence provenance, and visibility.

The remediation must preserve these distinctions:

- Wind Walker doctrine, situated role judgment, authenticated safety/authority
  grant, runtime effect, and durable replacement episode have different owners;
- a live packet references but does not own checkpoints, decisions, manifests,
  transition history, or outcome evidence;
- source origin, acquisition method, access, validity, freshness,
  applicability, uncertainty, conflict, and correction are independently
  representable;
- quantitative derivation is permitted only across compatible exact source
  observations and measurement epochs;
- projection access is consumer-scoped and least-disclosing, with a
  deny-by-default fresh-reviewer boundary;
- rehydration integrity cannot confer instruction authority;
- one builder exercise establishes bounded feasibility, not universal role
  applicability or permanent placement; and
- proposal formation cannot authorize an effectful reset or make one evidence
  route mandatory.

The revision should remove the causal dependency on the entire unaccepted
role-owned-state proposal. It may retain that candidate as a neighboring likely
owner while requiring only a minimal checkpoint, safety-attestation, manifest,
and durable-episode interface from whichever stronger owner an authorized
evaluation actually uses.

Any later effectful evaluation requires separate authority, a current safety
determination, authenticated inputs, and an external oracle or mechanically
checkable seeded obligation. Success can establish bounded falsification and
feasibility, not open-world completeness or causal outcome claims.

This synthesis is proposal-former judgment over advisory evidence. It does not
rewrite reviewer findings, accept the proposal, authorize implementation, or
settle permanent placement.

The resulting candidate-2 subject and retained same-session re-evaluation are
recorded in [`remediation-results.md`](remediation-results.md). All initial
findings were verified resolved at proposal-contract level; the non-acceptance
and non-implementation boundaries above remain unchanged.
