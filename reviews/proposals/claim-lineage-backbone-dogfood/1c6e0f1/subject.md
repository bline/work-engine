## Review boundary

Each specialist begins fresh and read-only with respect to the proposal. The
specialists may write only their own review artifact. They do not receive other
reviewers' findings or coordinator synthesis before their initial judgment.
Their outputs are diagnostic and advisory; they cannot mutate the subject,
accept or reject the proposal, change the parent decision, settle placement, or
authorize dogfood implementation.

If remediation is warranted, the same retained specialist should receive the
exact new immutable subject, bounded delta, its own prior findings, and the
proposal former's concise remediation consequence. Such a pass is a retained
continuation, not a new independent review.
