## Findings

### 1. Material — the refresh proof records authority but does not establish who can exercise it

The fixture contract records the producer and actual authority scope of each
*original* judgment, and the evidence report records the authority that produced
each original and refresh judgment
(`claim-lineage-backbone-dogfood/proposal.md:88-105,140-152`). But the proof then
relies on an “authorized refresh judgment,” and placement says such a judgment
owns reconciliation, without naming the authority source or requiring the later
execution disposition to bind it for each fixture
(`proposal.md:67-78,212-219`; `placement.md:22-34`). An authority reference is
provenance; its presence does not prove that its producer could reinterpret or
refresh a historical proposal-research claim or specialist finding. Governing
doctrine makes authority and approval boundaries invariant and limits model
judgment to authority actually granted (`DESIGN.md:21-46,495-507`).

This matters especially because the parent says domain contracts name refresh
and assessment roles, unresolved ownership must not be inferred from possession
of a packet, validator, planner, coordinator, or supervisor role, and producing
an assessment does not confer reliance or transition authority
(`claim-centered-evidence-lineage/proposal.md:383-407`). The parent also says the
proposal-research workflow is not yet formed (`placement.md:18-21`). Accepting or
executing this dogfood proposal therefore cannot silently supply missing
research-maintenance authority, and implementation-completion authority remains
limited to nomination (`claim-centered-evidence-lineage/relationships.md:30-42`).

Required consequence: before execution can claim either refresh proof, the
authority-owned disposition or bound fixture contract must identify, for each
fixture, (a) the source authority for the original judgment, (b) the authority
for the dogfood-local claim-boundary interpretation, (c) the authority for the
terminal refresh judgment and whether it is only experimental or changes a
domain-owned canonical judgment, and (d) the downstream owner of reliance and
consumer reopening. Missing authority must remain unresolved. The fixture
selector, implementation producer, schema validator, projection, proof runner,
and advisory recommender must not acquire any of those authorities by producing
or possessing durable records.

### 2. Moderate — positive proof coverage and truthful experiment validity are worded as one “success” state

The proposal says the dogfood “succeeds only if” one exercise demonstrates all
four proofs and requires both `retained_unchanged` and changed terminal paths
(`proposal.md:46-74`). It later correctly says negative or ambiguous results are
valid evidence and proof conditions must not be weakened (`proposal.md:200-210`).
Those statements can coexist, but the single success label creates pressure to
select or interpret fixtures so both desired outcomes appear. Pre-binding
fixtures prevents post-production substitution and has an explicit causal
parent—cherry-picking risk (`proposal.md:88-114`)—but it does not by itself make a
role's anticipated or prior judgment independent evidence. Doctrine requires a
consequence to describe the protected terminal property rather than reintroduce
a procedure through outcome wording (`DESIGN.md:48-62,184-201`).

Required consequence: distinguish a valid, truthfully executed experiment from
a positive four-proof result. Pre-bound fixtures that yield only one terminal
path, an unresolved outcome, or an authority gap should produce valid negative
or incomplete evidence, not a failed exercise to be repaired by manufacturing
the missing judgment. Both terminal paths may remain necessary before a later
authority concludes that the shared minimum is positively demonstrated.

## Supported consequences

- Formation, review, evaluation, decision, and implementation authorization
  remain distinct. The packet is formed for later evaluation, does not inherit
  acceptance from the parent's semantic-review closure, and requires a later
  revision-bound authority disposition before execution (`proposal.md:3-18,
  35-44,212-219`; `adaptive-specialized-review-panels.md:37-56,167-195`).
- Original judgment ownership, impact nomination, refresh judgment, reliance,
  consumer reopening, and advisory recommendation are substantially separated.
  Completion can nominate but cannot decide semantic change; projection owns no
  meaning or causality; downstream owners retain reliance/reopening; and the
  evidence-report recommendation is explicitly advisory (`proposal.md:59-86,
  128-155`; `placement.md:22-34`; `relationships.md:34-43`).
- The anti-circular rule is explicit and repeated: durable records and passing
  validation do not establish truth or architectural correctness, the role may
  not treat its durable prior judgment as independent support, and dogfood
  feedback cannot automatically revise or decide the parent (`proposal.md:30-33,
  107-114,197-210`; `relationships.md:3-13`; `family.md:66-72`). This matches the
  governing review doctrine that persistence preserves provenance rather than
  epistemic or decision authority (`adaptive-specialized-review-panels.md:
  219-231,252-263`).
- Most strong commands have causal parents or protect real boundaries: fixture
  pre-binding addresses cherry-picking; independent readability and provenance
  protect reconstructability; non-destructive ownership prevents semantic
  takeover; and truthful negative results protect evidence integrity. I found
  no contract conflict caused merely by the proposed Git-local realization.
