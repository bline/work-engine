## Panel consequence

The panel supports the proposal's central boundary after remediation:

- implementation review is the primary consumer of the specialist because it
  provides actual agent-facing instruction surfaces;
- proposal review selects it only for present normative instruction content,
  loading or precedence contracts, or exact routes claimed as mandatory;
- the diagnostic questions are shared design reasoning while finding ownership
  follows the diagnosed subject;
- architecture, doctrine-authority, seam review, skill authoring, panel
  coordination, proposal formation, and acceptance retain separate ownership;
- findings distinguish binding design contracts from advisory philosophy or
  another named authority source; and
- the implemented candidate cannot provide its own bootstrap certification.

## Review selection

Architecture/placement, doctrine/authority, and lifecycle/applicability had
direct decision value for the formed proposal. The proposed instruction
reviewer remained omitted because no implemented instruction surface was in the
subject. UI and implementation review remained inapplicable. Seam review was
omitted because the subject introduced no actual cross-subsystem handoff; its
selection becomes justified when a later proposal or implementation creates
one.
