## Selected panel

Fresh architecture/placement, doctrine/authority, and
review-lifecycle/applicability specialists evaluate the subject independently.

- Architecture/placement tests whether a distinct specialist and new proposal
  family are justified, whether the probable skill and consumer placement is
  coherent, and whether rejected owners remain truthfully separate.
- Doctrine/authority tests the two-question reduction, exact-route causality,
  philosophy-versus-binding-contract treatment, review authority, and the
  self-certification boundary.
- Review lifecycle/applicability tests conditional proposal selection, primary
  implementation consumption, immutable subjects, remediation continuity,
  omission truth, bootstrap independence, and prospective artifact/state use.

The proposed agent-instruction reviewer is intentionally omitted. This subject
defines candidate outcomes and boundaries but contains no implemented
`SKILL.md`, prompt, role profile, or other operational instruction surface.
Selecting the candidate reviewer merely because the proposal anticipates a
future skill would contradict its own applicability boundary and create a
self-validation loop.

UI, accessibility, and visual review are omitted because the subject creates no
present interface consequence. Implementation review is omitted because no
implementation is authorized or included.

Each specialist receives the exact subject, governing doctrine, and its scoped
questions without another specialist's conclusions or coordinator synthesis.
Findings are diagnostic and advisory. Persistence does not make them evidence
for themselves or exercise proposal decision authority.
