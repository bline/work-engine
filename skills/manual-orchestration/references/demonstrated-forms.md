# Demonstrated manual forms

These are observed S10 examples, not required schemas or proof of complete decomposition. Use the smallest fields sufficient for the actual claim and keep authoritative contract text with its owner.

| Form and producer | Exact demonstrated example | Decision-bearing content |
| --- | --- | --- |

B was split by independently verifiable results. |
| Worker packet, coordinator/supervisor | [`C2/packet-v1.md`](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/slices/C2/packet-v1.md) | Authority hashes, current candidate and relevant prior acceptance, actual schema-7 subject, six owner obligations, checks, process and mutation boundaries, return artifacts. |
| Worker result, worker | [`C2/result-v3.json`](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/slices/C2/result-v3.json) | Remediation packet, bindings, selected-v3 schema route, positive and omission evidence, tests, failed proof artifacts, process disposition and limits. Its status requests supervisor review, not acceptance. |

| Private readiness without integration | [`D/supervisor-result-v1.json`](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/slices/D/supervisor-result-v1.json) | Exact manifest/review/check references, three unqualified TO2 tests, 17-path prior-byte limit, root as next integration owner. |

Useful optional projections across these forms: `path` plus SHA256 and producer; actual selected binary/profile/schema and source subject; property, relevant states, observations and bounded exhaustiveness basis when a semantic obligation is assessed; limitations and invalidated assumptions; failed and passed check references; requested versus observed identity; open process or child owner; next decision owner. Include a field because the claim needs it, not to fill a generic ledger. No example above establishes a complete obligation inventory or automatic planning accuracy.
