# Organization formation

Load this reference when forming or revising a reviewable organization, including
during ongoing implementation with no new dispatch, launch or discovery
assignment, or when assigned discovery for such a candidate. It
extends the existing JSON-state plus Markdown-packet convention; it is not a
runtime manager or an implementation grant. Ordinary bounded assignments do not
need a separate organization artifact.

## Discovery and a reviewable candidate

The coordinator assembles the whole candidate against the objective and authority
map. A supervisor explores its bounded branch and proposes children where they
make coherent outcomes assignable. An implementer assigned discovery reports its
findings, ownership boundaries and whether it can own a coherent implementation
outcome. A justified leaf is a valid branch disposition. Depth, fan-out and the
order of exploration remain judgment.

Discovery launches require currently authorized discovery packets and explicit
model, effort, tier and launch selections before launch. A discovery acceptance
does not authorize implementation. Stop discovery when the branch is defensibly
assignable, more exploration has no credible decision value, or an explicit
limit or authority boundary prevents further work. Record the remaining
uncertainty and its owner so that a reviewable candidate need not pretend all
questions are settled.

Give the organization and each logical role stable identities and revision the
candidate. The [illustrative JSON form](../assets/organization-candidate.example.json)
shows bounded outcomes and ownership, logical `reports_to`, branch findings,
proposed children or a justified leaf, evidence, uncertainty, requested exact
model/effort/tier, launch choice and proposed retention. Adapt the form to the
claim; it has no executable consumer or mandatory schema. Pair it with the
existing Markdown packets where detailed authority and checks belong.

Link launched discovery roles to their current assignment and continuation
records. Those records own observed session/rollout, configuration, runtime
ancestry and active result/process collection bindings. Keep these actual
bindings there rather than copying a second runtime ledger into the candidate.
An unlaunched proposal has no observed session merely because a role is named.

## Selection and useful continuity

Include proposed implementation and review selections in the candidate, then
check the active user or contract selection before dispatch. Use the
[model guide](../../../docs/model-selection.md) for selection guidance and the
applicable [Codex](codex-host.md) or [Anthropic](anthropic-host.md) host for launch
and observation mechanics; their defaults remain with those owners. Record
requested and observed identity/tier separately in the assignment. An unavailable
identity is unknown; a contract-bound mismatch is unmet. An unavailable selected
model supplies no authority for silent substitution.

A useful discovery supervisor or implementer can continue into authorized
implementation after the existing scoped [context check](context-boundary.md).
Record the retain-or-fresh choice and reason in its assignment. Durable findings
make the candidate reviewable without access to the retained conversation;
retention preserves useful operational understanding. An initial independent
review starts from a separately established context, apart from discovery,
design and building; the owning review claim determines what isolation and
evidence are required. A proposed reviewer selection does not establish actual
model, tier or independence.

## Revision decisions and authority

The user's organization revision boundary is coordinator approval for small
changes and human approval for large changes. The coordinator classifies a
change by its consequences and explains the basis; no fixed size or role-count
rule decides it. Existing provider, budget, mutation, review and other explicit
approval boundaries retain precedence even for a small change. Initial candidate
acceptance belongs to the approval owner named by the active task, without a
blanket initial human gate. Organization acceptance never grants new task,
launch or mutation authority.

Approval of one set of bytes cannot establish approval of changed scope or
selections. Freeze each candidate revision before its decision and bind that
decision, in the existing assignment or decision record, to its exact path and
SHA-256, prior revision, change classification and basis, decision owner and
source approval or authority evidence. Keep approval outside the candidate so
the digest is not self-referential. For a pending decision, record it as pending
without inventing approval evidence. Any changed candidate needs its own
applicability decision; unchanged accepted portions can carry forward only with
an explicit reason why the prior approval still covers those portions. Preserve
the old subject and decision as history.

Logical `reports_to` describes work reporting. It does not rewrite runtime
ancestry, change a notification route or transfer process/result collection.
Before relying on a reporting revision, preserve a verified collection owner
and delivery path for every outstanding result and owned process in the current
assignment. Actual replacement or transfer follows the existing host and
[context contracts](context-boundary.md); changing a candidate edge alone cannot
perform it. This prevents a logically tidy organization from stranding live work.
