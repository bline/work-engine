# Supervisor: one engineering group

Own a bounded group from packet receipt through worker collection, final evidence checks, required retained review, and a compact result to the coordinator. Bind current affected bytes when work begins - an [evidence battery](../../evidence-battery/SKILL.md) plan over the affected and dirty paths, saved for later `--compare`; an admission HEAD can be stale or omit dirty work. Verify the actual subject the worker exercised: source revision, launch path, binary/profile, store schema and selected input/output matter when the claim depends on them. A true observation of a narrower subject remains useful history, but cannot satisfy the assigned subject. Inspect material failures and their cause before acceptance; preserve unsuccessful logs and corrected-route lineage.

## Launch

Before launching a builder or reviewer, record its task profile as **Task profile (shadow pilot)** in [coordinator](coordinator.md#packets) describes; declare yourself as `supervisor`.

Assign one coherent outcome to a worker with explicit file/process ownership and the current packet revision. Tell it to load this skill as worker: invoke `$manual-orchestration`, or read `skills/manual-orchestration/SKILL.md` when the skill is not installed. After launch, verify its session and send its identity binding as [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md#launching-a-child) describes. Compare the requested model and effort with the launch configuration promptly; a mismatch needs an explicit disposition before that worker does engineering, and its consumed work remains part of accounting. When a child session identity matters for handoff, review continuity or measurement, bind it to actual runtime lineage; an inherited shell thread ID may name the parent. Distinguish launch configuration and recorded turn context from backend identity if the latter is not exposed.

## Next task, remediation and handoff

Before giving a retained worker a substantial new task - **including remediation after review** - run the pre-dispatch check in [context boundary](context-boundary.md) and record retain or fresh with its reason. The trigger is a pilot hypothesis (H1), and you may overrule its recommendation with a recorded reason. For a finished builder over the trigger, you write the remediation packet from its result, continuation and the review findings, and give it to a fresh worker. If work is unfinished, receive a durable continuation naming live ownership, decisions, failures, evidence pointers, remaining obligations and next action. Check it is safe to transfer, then follow the transfer steps in that reference. Completion notification wakes collection; it does not certify success. Collect all required worker/reviewer results and account for processes before returning to the coordinator.

## Validation and review

Validate the result against the owning contract and actual required gates, not merely the packet's checklist. Run required gates and validate affected downstream consumers, including those reached through shared contracts and schemas; shared-boundary changes require joined-workspace qualification (an S8 schema bump broke 14 S7 tests that the changed package's checks did not exercise). Report unavailable or incomplete gates explicitly. Obtain retained review where the owner requires it. Preserve the review episode and actual continuity: if a reviewer runtime is lost, an authorized reconstructed successor is a reconstructed continuation, not fresh independence or `same_session`. Do not write a review-state transition that falsifies continuity. An immutable external report may support bounded semantic acceptance when that owner permits it; disclose absent state linkage and refer infrastructure repair to its owner. Review-state persistence, semantic acceptance and integration have different owners.

## Result

Return a bound supervisor result stating the exact accepted or stopped scope, validation and reviewer evidence, limitations, open findings, requested/observed identities, context checks and dispatch decisions, child collection and process disposition, and the next owner. A private readiness finding is a decision input for the coordinator. Never infer full campaign acceptance from it.
