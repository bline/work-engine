# v2c final forensic closure

## Decision

The closure gate is `traceability_with_candidate_downgrade`.

Exact source recovery closes the quotation-survival gap cleanly. v2c may now support the abstraction claim within its existing same-family and sample-size boundaries. Shaw remains a valid profile-traceability manipulation but is downgraded as the lead behavioral candidate because one licensed interrogative discourse cue is sufficient across both briefs.

## Quotation survival

All six original source objects and all three corpus manifests matched their retained pre-extraction SHA-256 digests. Sixteen renders were compared against all six sources: 96 comparisons total.

- Longest exact normalized-token overlap across all comparisons: 4 tokens.
- Exact matches at the preregistered material threshold of 8 tokens: 0.
- Near-verbatim matches of 12–30 tokens at ≥90% positional identity: 0.
- Source→profile/style-card→render mediated matches of at least 5 tokens: 0.

Therefore the quotation gate is `no_material_overlap`. The audit found no evidence that v2c measured source-text retrieval, compression, or near-verbatim survival.

## Cue concentration

Assignment denominators recompute to 12 for each condition, not 16: four samples multiplied by three passes.

Only Shaw has a one-feature rule that remains perfect in each brief and across the full observed set:

```text
question_mark_count > 0 → Shaw
```

Full-set confusion counts are 4 true positives, 12 true negatives, zero false positives, and zero false negatives. All 12 Shaw rationales identify questions as the primary organizing cue, while citing a median of one additional intended feature family.

No cross-brief-perfect scalar rule was found for Gelman, Leveson, or neutral. Their best full-sample trivial baselines were imperfect and failed to transfer perfectly between briefs.

## Final v2c interpretation

The exact-source result removes the prior corpus-quotation qualification. The earned statement is:

> Under a same-family Sol apparatus, semantically accepted outputs retained sufficient intended profile signal for perfect blinded profile traceability across the observed sample set, without material exact, near-verbatim, or source-mediated quotation survival under the preregistered checks.

This remains an upstream manipulation result—not independent recognizability or behavioral activation. The Shaw result is real profile traceability, and guiding questions may themselves induce inspection, alternative generation, or premise challenging. Because one salient discourse cue is sufficient, however, this run cannot distinguish surface imitation, explicitly cued epistemic behavior, and broader Shaw-register activation. Shaw is therefore a poor lead for the main behavioral pilot unless interrogative syntax is ablated or equalized.

This closes the v2c forensic campaign. Further work should be a new behavioral or ablation experiment with its own preregistration, not another retrospective audit of v2c.
