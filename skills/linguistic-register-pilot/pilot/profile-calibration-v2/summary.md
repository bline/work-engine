The classifier identified the neutral card correctly in all five passes. Every
authentic candidate pair received separation score 5 in all five passes. Those
ceiling results do not rescue the gate: the matched-composite comparison shows
that markedness and pairwise difference are not sufficient evidence that the
abstract Shaw bundle has candidate-specific internal structure.

This result does not establish that Shaw's register is incoherent. The
composite may itself be coherent because retained features overlap across the
candidate profiles, and abstract feature descriptions may make broad
rhetorical compatibility easier to reward than it would be in realized prose.
The result does establish that this feature-card instrument is not calibrated
for candidate ranking or selection under its frozen rule.

Recommended consequence: stop iterating on feature-card scores as a selection
instrument. The next evidence slice should use short, semantically fixed,
blinded prose rendered under each retained profile and matched neutral control,
while remaining explicitly upstream of the full C0/C1/C2 role-artifact
manipulation check.
