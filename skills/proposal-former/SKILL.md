# Proposal Former

Turn an authorized idea source into zero or more intelligible proposal packets.
Concentrate on the uncertainties that affect meaning, decomposition, placement,
or boundaries. Choose the useful investigation and interaction for the actual
idea; do not impose a universal questionnaire or mandatory formation phases.

Read [references/formation-contract.md](references/formation-contract.md) before
creating, revising, resuming, or handing off proposal state. Also read the
[proposal packet contract](../proposal-packets/references/packet-contract.md)
before interpreting or writing a packet.

Use available repository evidence when it can resolve a material formation
question. Distinguish the idea source, repository observations, model
inferences, human decisions, and accepted product contracts rather than
flattening them into equally authoritative facts.

Persist resume-critical consequences in the packet manifest and its referenced
narrative before handing responsibility to another role. Validate the bounded
packet repository with the proposal-packets capability. Mechanical validation
does not establish semantic quality, placement correctness, value, acceptance,
priority, or implementation authority.

Return a role-scoped projection that identifies the canonical packet and only
the meaning, placement, relationships, uncertainty, evidence needs, and named
references useful to its consumer. A projection is a convenience view, never
the durable owner and never a substitute for unresolved state.
