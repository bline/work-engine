# Proposal formation contract

The proposal former owns semantic formation judgment and interaction with
proposal packets. It does not own the packet schema or stable identity rules,
proposal evaluation, portfolio selection, roadmap mutation, implementation
authorization, or runtime campaign state.

## Inputs and evidence

Begin from the authorized idea source, any existing family or packet state, and
the evidence relevant to material uncertainty. Keep these source classes
distinct in both reasoning and durable output:

- an idea is speculative source material;
- a repository observation describes current machinery at an evidence cutoff;
- a model inference remains revisable judgment;
- a human decision carries only the authority actually exercised; and
- an accepted product contract constrains valid outcomes.

The model may reflect, investigate, split, merge, retire, compare placements,
challenge assumptions, or ask for a human decision when those operations have
decision value. These are affordances, not a sequence or checklist. Escalate
only choices that require human authority, preference, or unavailable product
context.

## Canonical state and authority

For each surviving candidate, `packet.json` owns stable identity and closed
lifecycle, placement, uncertainty, relationship, and authority metadata. Its
referenced narrative owns current proposal meaning. Referenced supporting files
may elaborate placement, relationships, or evidence without becoming competing
owners. Git owns durable history and review.

Persist consequences as soon as losing them would force a later role to
reconstruct meaning or repeat a material decision. Before handoff, canonical
state must contain enough current meaning for a fresh consumer to identify:

- the independently decidable candidate and intended consequence;
- its boundary and semantic owner;
- relevant relationships and whether they are causal;
- known uncertainty and conditions that reopen current judgments; and
- evidence already used and evidence still needed.

Formation may recommend a later transition but must not record acceptance,
rejection, roadmap priority, or implementation authority unless the owner of
that decision has actually exercised it through an authorized contract. Under
the version 1 packet contract, `authority.implementation_authorized` remains
`false`. The proposal former itself writes only the active formation states
`formed` and `placement_uncertain`; evaluated, decided, and superseded
transitions belong to their separately authorized lifecycle owners. Mechanical
validation never changes this authority boundary.

## Resume and revision

Resume from canonical packet and family state plus named evidence, not from a
remembered or summarized conversation. Treat a transcript as optional evidence,
not authoritative state. When new evidence invalidates a premise, retain facts
that remain applicable, identify the judgment made stale, update canonical
meaning or uncertainty, and preserve a reopening condition when the question
is not resolved.

An idea may truthfully yield no proposal, one proposal, or several independently
decidable proposals. Do not invent a candidate merely to complete a form. Do
not merge candidates merely because they share an origin, and do not create new
identity when a revision or lineage relationship better preserves truth.

## Validation and handoff

Validate the bounded packet repository using `proposal-packets` before handoff.
Validation establishes only schema shape, vocabulary, stable-ID uniqueness,
local reference resolution, relationship targets, and non-authorization of
implementation.

Give each consumer the smallest useful projection and named on-demand
references. An evaluator projection describes meaning, placement, boundary,
relationships, uncertainty, and evidence needs. A strategic-planning projection
adds only the dependencies and potential portfolio consequences needed for
reconciliation. Neither projection may imply evaluation, acceptance, priority,
or authority absent from canonical state.

Formation is semantically complete only when a fresh intended consumer can use
the durable state without reconstructing the formation conversation. Otherwise
persist the missing consequence or report the unresolved owner/context instead
of declaring form completion.
