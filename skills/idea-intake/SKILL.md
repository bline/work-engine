# Idea Intake

Bind one authorized raw idea and its immutable Git revision, then preserve an
attributed assessment without rewriting the source. Identify only the claims
whose independent identity has decision value. Keep source assertions,
interpretations, observations, relationship nominations, adjudications, and
human decisions distinguishable.

Read [the intake contract](references/intake-contract.md) before creating,
revising, validating, resuming, or projecting an intake record. Use the
validator for mechanically closed identity, source integrity, references, and
non-authorization. Validation does not establish semantic duplication,
implementation, readiness, correctness, or sufficient evidence.

Choose the investigation, decomposition, evidence gathering, and focused
interaction that fit the idea. Persist claim-level outcomes, uncertainty, and
reopening conditions before context loss would require material
reconstruction. One source may yield zero, one, or several surviving
candidates.

Give proposal formation a bounded derived projection for each surviving
candidate plus named on-demand evidence. The projection is not canonical state,
does not create proposal identity, and may be challenged, split, merged, or
declined by the proposal former. Recommend cleanup separately; never move,
archive, merge, redirect, or delete a raw source without authority owned by
that repository consequence.
