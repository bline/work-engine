# Executable invariants (`pi-accounting-contract/1.4-s1`)

There are three families, kept apart: **financial** (stages C to X: money facts), **attribution** (stage T) and
**admission** (stage R and the admission facts). Each entry gives:
- the statement, and its preconditions where they matter;
- the observable result and its corpus coverage (case ids under `corpus/cases`; the validator checks every invariant
  has at least one case);
- the generator property for S2 (proptest), and the smallest implementation mutation that must violate the invariant.

Corpus cases cite the invariants they exercise. P1.27's INV-1 to INV-11 are refined here into F1 to F11, with T1 to T5
and A1 to A6 added. Revision 3 adds F12, A7 and A8 and restates F1, F3, F5, A1, A3, A4, A5 and A6. Revision 4 adds A9 and A10 and restates A1, A3, A5, A7 and A8. Revision 5 adds F13 and restates F3, F5 and A9.

## Admission invariants (stage R)

| ID | Statement | Passing | Violating | Corpus | Property | Mutation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A1 Valid controls admit** | Evidence with an established owner, settled billing, no `AtLeast` commitment and headroom is admitted, counted **per capability profile**. Under `current_producer` only sessionless or Codex cases can meet it (OQ-1); `future_qualified_gate` cases with qualified gates are the metered positive controls. A reject-everything implementation fails both | current: VC08, N03 controls, V03-control; future: P132-FG-* positives | an implementation that blocks everything | per-profile minimums (validator) | — | return `not_established` unconditionally (self-test `always_reject`) |
| **A2 Established excludes unresolved exposure** | Any `AtLeast` in an objective's summed lines or commitments, any blocking finding in its scope, or an uncertain closure gives `NotEstablished` | N01, D4, N03 | admitting on a known partial amount | 6 cases | — | ignore `AtLeast` in the rollup |
| **A3 Commitments never increase headroom** | Malformed, negative, conflicting or reopened commitment records give `NotEstablished` or keep the commitment. Open, pending, retired-resumable and reopened commitments are `AtLeast` unless a qualified gate decides otherwise (A-5). Retirement alone releases nothing (L-7), and a reopening never releases (A-4) | S08, N07×2, P130-R10, P132-FG-retired-released | a -1 cap (S-08); any retire releasing (N-07); a retired current-producer session releasing (OQ-1) | 10 cases | random journal corruption never lowers Σ committed below the valid reading | release the commitment when any retire row exists |
| **A4 Predecessor obligations, no double count** | A successor's predecessor line = the high of every predecessor attempt whose billing is unresolved (outstanding, or closed with `AtLeast`) + open commitments; settled (billing-established) predecessor spend is excluded; predecessor closure discharges nothing; no attempt is in two owned lines | VC15, D1-successor, P130-R07 | charging the successor with settled spend; dropping a closed-but-unresolved attempt (P1.29 R-07) | 3 cases | settle a predecessor attempt with a usage bound: it leaves the successor line; close it on an upstream error: it stays | include `owned_closed` in the line; select by obligation state instead of billing |
| **A5 Monotone admission** | Adding non-settlement evidence never turns a denial (`refused` or `not_established`, equally non-permissive) into `admitted`, **except** an enumerated permitted release: a valid dated retirement **together with a qualified gate** (A-3), or a valid `released` admission row (A-2). A retirement under the current producer is not a permitted release (OQ-1). `refused` and `not_established` may change into each other (OQ-2: added evidence can raise the known total) | V06, R05, A1-03, N02, N05, N07; permitted: P132-FG pair | dropping unreadable evidence; treating a current-producer retirement as a release | 13 cases | random base plus random non-settlement evidence other than a permitted release: never denial → admitted | treat unreadable lines as absent |
| **A6 Producer-supported omissions keep the settlement valid** | A producer-supported omission does not reject the settlement. Null OpenAI cache counts are valued over the split vertices and can admit. A missing OpenAI tier keeps a `Valid` settlement but has no usage bound, so it is `AtLeast` (R-03). A null Anthropic tier is standard (R-14) | N06, P130-R14 (admitted); U08 (valid, `AtLeast`) | rejecting the settlement (spurious fail-closed); pricing a missing tier as finite | 3 cases | — | require cached tokens present; reject a null Anthropic tier |
| **A7 Closure precedence** | An established closure gives `refused` before any monetary or accounting evaluation; a claimed but unestablished closure gives `not_established`. Current producer: a consistent `scope_budget close` entry matching `closed_at` (legacy cooperative trust; `set_by` proves nothing). Future vertical: an authority closure record. Closure never discharges predecessor obligations (§12.4, Q11, OQ-4) | P132-CP-closed-valid-close-entry (refused); P132-FG-closure-authority (refused) | timestamp-only closure (D1-closed, U04×2: not established); a close entry contradicting `closed_at`; a legacy close entry in the vertical profile | 8 cases | random closure evidence: established → refused whatever the accounting | apply the limit test before the closure test |
| **A8 Unenforced commitments block** | A commitment without a qualified gate deciding release or a billing bound is `AtLeast` and blocks (A-5); current-producer retired sessions stay `retired_resumable` (OQ-1) | P132-FG-retired-released and P132-FG-open-billing-bound (admitted) | VC01 and the other current-producer retired controls (`retired_resumable`); a self-asserted gate | 20+ cases | any commitment without a qualified gate: `AtLeast` | `kind = exact` for retired or launched-open commitments |
| **A9 Gate qualification** | A gate counts only if authority, identity, scope, period, mechanisms, receipts and consistency all hold (G-1..G-7), and a billing-bound gate also binds a numerical USD amount no greater than the cap, covering forwarding, retries, partial streams, failures and settlement, with a matching binding digest (G-8, A-03); it changes no attempt money | P132-FG-retired gated twins; P133-FG-VC14-authenticated-bound | self-asserted, wrong authority, wrong session, request scope, expired, missing mechanisms or receipts, contradicted; bound missing, above the cap, wrong currency, incomplete coverage, binding mismatch | 15 cases | random gate field corruption never qualifies a gate | trust `gated: true`; trust a bound flag without an amount |
| **A10 Known over-limit refusal** | When the known total of owned amounts, valid commitments, attributable predecessor obligations and the new request exceeds the limit, the decision is `refused` even with unresolved billing; reasons are unchanged; candidate (uncertain or unadmitted) amounts are never attributed (OQ-2) | D1-successor, VC14 limit-0.55, P132-CP-known-over-limit (refused) | P132-CP-unknown-below-limit (known portion below the limit: `not_established`) | 20+ cases | raising a known amount never turns refused into admitted | return `not_established` whenever any term is `AtLeast` |
