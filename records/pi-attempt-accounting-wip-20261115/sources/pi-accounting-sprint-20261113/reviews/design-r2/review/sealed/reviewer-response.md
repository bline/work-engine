## Bounded correction plan

### 1. Establish the owning decisions

For user disposition and root adoption, specify:

- Monetary component selection has one owner; lane/pool facts cannot erase retained amounts.
- Only an eligible usage-bound witness permits reservation replacement.
- Complete, established, fully priced multiidentity bounds remain eligible; findings never decide money.
- R owns financial provisionality. State whether predecessor exposure and commitments participate; distinguish temporal completeness if consumers need both.
- Shared reason catalogues define vocabulary, not competing finding-emission owners.

Preserve the rejected candidate, predecessor history and independent expectations.

### 2. Strengthen declarations without building a reducer

Use fixed, result-bearing records for financial branches and projections. Guards should reference actual typed facts and applicability witnesses, rather than unexplained labels such as `exposure_fact`.

Require:

- every legal declared input combination has exactly one outcome;
- exclusions have explicit contractual justification;
- every monetary reference is available on its branch;
- every reservation-backed ineligible branch retains R;
- every external state, field, reason and cross-field constraint has a declared projection;
- tables and interfaces are generated from these records, not separately authored normative strings.

Arithmetic can remain single-owned text with typed signatures and independently checked examples. Structural checks need not calculate arbitrary raw-attempt outcomes.

### 3. Repair causal interfaces and dependencies

Declare primary-key grouping, then a single-owned settlement-validity witness, then validity-dependent auxiliary attachment. Establish that auxiliary evidence cannot alter that witness’s inputs. S consumes the witness rather than duplicating its policy; execution/forwarding can consume enriched evidence.

Separate C–K evidence findings from L/T/R findings. Register named-type, predicate, formula, projection and residual-consumer dependencies. Do not mistake registration itself for semantic completeness.

### 4. Require decisive qualification

Before reconsidering freeze, require independent witnesses and negative controls demonstrating:

- contradicted lane plus R=500M cannot produce zero;
- incomplete or unpriceable usage retains R; eligible complete usage can replace it;
- complete priced multiidentity bounds remain finite while identity findings block;
- zero/conflicting reservations disable relative comparisons without suppressing applicable local failures;
- error-status usage survives without establishing billing;
- illegal external combinations and missing branches fail;
- retirement/closure cannot establish financial finality;
- writer-floor contradictions invalidate reachability claims;
- forged or altered mock payloads cannot qualify, and finite residual allocation cannot resolve `AtLeast` exposure.

Also qualify the validator against **self-consistent but semantically wrong expectations**, not only outputs differing from its existing answers. Keep raw oracle reasoning independent of canonical generation.

These checks remove competing decision owners and expose missing applicability, preventing the observed synchronization class rather than relocating its exceptions.

**D3 remains binding.** Static declaration checking, independent arithmetic assertions and attributed semantic review are feasible here. If acceptance instead requires executable full raw-input financial conformance during S1, the user must resolve that requirement against D3; neither an evaluator disguised as tooling nor a silently weakened claim is acceptable.

This is a design recommendation—not acceptance, history waiver, production authority, replacement modular clearance or authorization to edit.
