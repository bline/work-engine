## 3. Slice-1b review follow-ups
- **P3-1 (fixed, option better than (b))**: `numbers.rs` previously collapsed all wide integers to `±1e40` and collapsing fractions to `0.5`, making `uniqueItems` at `Assertion.expected_fields_or_relations` decide wrongly. The slice-1b differential parse test was adapted (non-integers compared by class). Documented limits: exponents beyond 2^40 in magnitude are clamped before classification; a diagnostic that prints a rejected non-integer shows its stand-in.
