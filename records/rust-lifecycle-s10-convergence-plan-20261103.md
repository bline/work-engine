## S10-F — Evidence feasibility and route decision

**Question:** can a bounded, admissible realization establish the remaining
required facts for the exact selected operation at a defensible cost?

Existing accepted maps, source dispositions and coverage are reusable evidence
inputs; refresh only what is missing or stale for this decision. Bind the
selected executable/profile, original unmet facts and their semantic owners.
For each still-missing fact, identify its producer, admissibility and observation
boundary, actual required access, and downstream consumer. Existing evidence
that already establishes a fact remains usable with its recorded limitations.

The uncertain capability needs a minimal actual demonstration under the
appropriate bounded acquisition grant. A proposed collector, compilation,
synthetic consumer test or empty violations list cannot establish that the
producer supplies the needed fact. Containment may help make the selected
profile tractable, but does not automatically establish independent history or
custody. Native history, Work Engine effect receipts and OS observations keep
their different evidentiary meanings.

Distinguish actual entry premises from evidence produced during an admitted
execution. Establish the required starting conditions and ability to acquire
the later observations; do not demand future effects or terminal settlement
as already-existing entry evidence. Conversely, a feasible acquisition plan
does not establish that those future observations occurred. S10-Q still needs
the actual run evidence before accepting its claim.

Exit with one attributable decision:

- **Feasible:** identify the admissible producer/consumer chain, retain the
  discriminating demonstration and its limits, and give a finite plan through
  S10-Q with its required reviews, builds, qualification attempts and authority.
  This establishes route feasibility, not the full production claim.
- **Authority-blocked:** name the exact missing access or owner decision and
  why it prevents a discriminating observation. Stop paid waiting; do not label
  an untested mechanism technically infeasible.
- **Infeasible within the selected profile, or inconclusive at the cap:** name
  the failed premise or missing demonstration. Present the smallest credible
  profile, realization or acceptance alternatives with the claim each would
  preserve or change. Do not start another research/test slice automatically.

## Funding, scope changes and progress

Each stage assignment has one total cost ceiling, hard deadline and named
exit. Its forecast includes the full remaining outcome: preparation, all
participants, validation, any expected recovery/remediation, complete required
review, collection and closing calls. Child assignments consume that allocation;
new sessions, micro-slices and retries do not renew the clock or cost. Existing
closed allocations and consumed attempts remain closed.

Reserve enough for a complete review and closure using measured costs. Missing
funding or capability yields a held result before dependent work; forecasts
are not evidence that the result is already complete. If an existing grant
contains phase caps, preserve them unless its funding owner changes them.
No silent phase transfer or historical recharge is introduced by this plan.

New findings may require legitimate repairs. When they change the selected
claim, evidence feasibility, critical dependency, or ability to finish within
the allocation, the stage owner returns the changed consequence and options
to the decision owner. Ordinary in-scope fixes and diagnostics retain their
existing authorization. At the cap, collect owned work and report the exact
accepted, failed, blocked or inconclusive standing. There is no automatic next
grant or review/recovery loop.

Additional tests need a named unmet acceptance condition or concrete defect
and a consequence for the admitted qualification route. Test count, another
accepted mapping, and spend below a per-item cap are not completion measures.
Preserve diagnostic failures and prior acceptance instead of rerunning until
green or repeatedly reconstructing the same source map.
