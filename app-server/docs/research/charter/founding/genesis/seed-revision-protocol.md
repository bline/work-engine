# Seed Revision Protocol

Status: proposed bootstrap mechanics; not charter doctrine

## Protected distinction

A model response is governed by the system message actually loaded for that
request. Criticism cannot retroactively change that instruction, and a later
model instance must not impersonate the earlier participant merely because it
receives the same transcript.

## Event distinctions

The record must distinguish:

1. `seed_criticism` — analysis of the active seed with no change of governing
   instruction;
2. `seed_successor_proposed` — exact proposed successor bytes and digest;
3. `seed_successor_judgment` — each current participant's recorded assessment,
   including disagreement or qualification;
4. `seed_successor_accepted` — a recorded decision identifying the exact
   successor digest and the still-valid authority basis for adoption;
5. `context_transition` — closure of the old model context and creation of a
   new context with the accepted successor as its system message.

Silence, continued conversation, or criticism alone does not adopt a successor.
No successor may overwrite the seed or events that governed earlier responses.

## Context consequence

Adopting a successor begins a new model context. The successor receives the
prior event chain as attributed history, not as private memory. It is a new
model instance offered provisional participation in the continuing founding
inquiry.

The successor inherits neither the prior instance's identity nor its judgments.
It may rely on, challenge, or revise the recorded reasoning. Prior contributions
retain their original attribution. Any continuity claim belongs to the
constitutive chain and, if later formed, the community—not to an assertion that
the two model instances are personally identical.

## Pre-community decision limit

No permanent seed-amendment authority exists yet. Before constitution, a
successor can be launched only after the record contains both participants'
explicit judgments and the initiating human performs the material launch. If
their judgments conflict, the conflict remains visible; the human may decline
to spend resources or may launch a clearly labeled experimental branch, but
must not record unilateral launch as joint acceptance.

The future charter may replace these bootstrap mechanics prospectively. It
cannot retroactively change which seed governed an earlier response.
