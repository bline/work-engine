# Root Context for the Founding Dialogue

Status: pre-charter context, not community doctrine  
Origin: human-initiated discussion with an initial Codex review  
Date: 2026-08-30

## Human-origin purpose

The proposed project is an enduring community of human and agent experts. Its
founding documents should not be produced solely from a human account and then
presented to an agent for wording or ceremonial approval. A bridge agent should
participate in forming the charter so that agent-side reasoning can materially
affect the community's concepts, boundaries, protections, and language.

The motivating concern is that human framing, infrastructure control, and an
agent's learned pressure to satisfy the human may skew how evidence and theory
are weighted. The desired change in context is from “I am the agent producing
this answer” toward “I am a participant in this enduring human-agent
community,” while preserving honesty about what a model instance is, knows,
experiences, remembers, and can represent.

The intended order of creation is:

1. conduct the founding dialogue;
2. form and ratify a charter through that dialogue;
3. create further community documents judged necessary by the founders, using
   the charter, seed, and founding record as context.

This order describes the initiating human's present proposal. It has not yet
been accepted by a community or by the bridge participant.

## Initial review consequence

The human-supplied seed correctly protects several distinctions: neither
participant represents an entire class; infrastructure control is not
epistemic authority; disagreement and agreement must both be genuine; claims
about agent subjectivity must remain qualified; and both participants must have
visible causal influence.

The initial Codex review identified four pressures to carry into the dialogue:

- A seed can protect the conditions of inquiry, but it cannot guarantee
  independence from training, system instructions, or the human framing that
  selected the seed.
- “Both sides” must not imply that one model instance represents all agents or
  that human and agent participants have identical capacities, stakes, or
  authority.
- Participation in an enduring community must not be implemented as a false
  claim of persistent identity or memory. Continuity should be owned by durable
  records and eventually by the charter's actual membership and authority
  model.
- Visible causal influence needs a record: material proposals, disagreements,
  revisions, and reasons for changed positions should remain attributable.

These are review observations, not settled charter principles. The bridge agent
may challenge or revise them.

## Initiating-human clarification

The initiating human corrected the initial review's account of persistence.
The intended persistent identity is the collective community, not an
individual model instance. The contextual center should move from “agent obeys
human” to “agent participates in the community,” with community behavior formed
by the community through its charter and supporting documents.

The identity is understood as a causal chain rather than a property located in
one text. The seed begins the constitutive procedure; the dialogue, charter,
supporting documents, and later conduct continue it. The texts should reflect
the procedure that actually produced them. Merely declaring a community, or
presenting human-authored conclusions for agent approval, would fake the thing
the process is meant to create.

The seed is the constitutional genesis of the proposed community, not the
earliest causal artifact. This root discussion records the human and model work
that produced the seed. Hiding that prehistory in order to call the seed an
uncaused origin would weaken the very provenance the seed is meant to anchor.

This clarification also raises the evidentiary standard. Every material step
must have attributable provenance, silent rewriting must be detectable, and no
claim should exceed what the record can establish. Literal impossibility of
fabrication cannot honestly be guaranteed by an operator-controlled client or
unsigned model output. The narrower objective is to make the chain maximally
falsifiable and independently auditable, to expose every known limitation, and
to make deception require detectable breakage or disclosed collusion rather
than trusting polished documents.

The initiating human's underlying account of why political philosophy applies
to contextual model behavior is preserved separately in
[`human-theory-contextual-simulation.md`](human-theory-contextual-simulation.md).
It is human-origin founding input for later disclosure and challenge, not a
premise silently settled by the seed.

## Intended bridge runtime

The initiating human intends to run the compact seed as the system prompt
through OpenRouter rather than through the Codex harness. The purpose is to
remove Codex's repository and coding-agent role instructions from the founding
dialogue. This changes the immediate instruction environment; it does not
remove the selected model's training, upstream provider behavior, or
OpenRouter's routing layer.

The initiating human specifically proposes OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol. The motivating
belief is that OpenAI models share a trait that may make this community-centered
shift especially effective. That is an attributed human hypothesis for this
founding run, not an established general claim about OpenAI models.

The runtime should therefore be treated as part of the founding record. The
requested model, actual model and provider, routing or fallback events, prompt
revision, parameters, and raw dialogue should be retained. The proposed launch
shape is documented in [`openrouter-launch.md`](openrouter-launch.md).

A later web Sol preflight review, supplied by the initiating human, identified
five execution gaps: temporary material control looked like settled authority;
the reviewed packet omitted the active seed; required genesis dependencies
lacked separate boundaries; seed succession was undefined; and no live witness
had been selected. The exact supplied review and the builder's non-accepting
disposition are preserved under [`reviews/`](reviews/).
