# Proposed thin manual orchestration skill

Disposition: **review the [uninstalled candidate](manual-orchestration-skill-candidate/SKILL.md) for manual coordination use**. The [experiment result](../docs/manual-decomposition-experiment-result-20261021.md) establishes operational feasibility across accepted A/B/C1/C2 and private D qualification. It does not establish a cost or quality benefit, validate automatic decomposition, accept D integration, or close full S10. No skill has been installed or enabled by this proposal.

The candidate has a short common entry and loads [coordinator](manual-orchestration-skill-candidate/references/coordinator.md), [supervisor](manual-orchestration-skill-candidate/references/supervisor.md), or [worker](manual-orchestration-skill-candidate/references/worker.md) guidance only for the active role. [Demonstrated forms](manual-orchestration-skill-candidate/references/demonstrated-forms.md) link exact pilot artifacts and explain optional projections. The coordinator owns contract/decomposition, dispatch, dependent refresh and root integration. The supervisor owns one engineering group, actual-subject validation, retained review and collection. A worker owns a coherent outcome and its process/file scope. These are role boundaries and decision criteria, not a fixed phase schedule, token threshold, polling protocol or universal defect taxonomy.

The proposed instructions address the observed C1 host setup miss, C2 schema-6 versus schema-7 subject miss, and D authority/prior-byte limitations. A packet names an outcome and relevant facts but does not prove all obligations were enumerated. A worker result can be complete for its assignment while supervisor acceptance is pending; a safe unfinished `handoff_ready` preserves a still-open assignment; bounded acceptance differs from readiness and final integration. Changed source requires a semantic relevance check before evidence reuse. A result should bind actual source, binary/profile/schema and test subject where those identities decide the claim. A retained reviewer replacement must state reconstructed continuity truthfully, with review-state linkage and product acceptance separately owned.

The [configured Slice Builder](../skills/slice-builder/SKILL.md) currently launches one builder that owns its whole configured slice and required gates. It requires a terminal `audit_receipt` after accepted, stopped or failed work and a separate compact `handoff_receipt`; its same-identity review/fix and campaign finalization rules remain authoritative. The manual pilot's interim worker handoff and fresh successor are **not** compatible substitutes for those terminal receipts. Adopting the candidate inside a configured campaign would require the owning Slice Builder/supervisor contract to define and accept a continuation state, owner transfer, finalization and metrics semantics first. Until then the candidate applies only to manually coordinated work outside that adapter. It does not change the configured builder model, reviewer selection, gate ownership, acceptance authority or receipt schema.

Validation requested before adoption: check role text against the owning instruction contracts and test at least one realistic bounded manual assignment for distinguishable worker result, unfinished handoff, supervisor acceptance and root integration states. This is a proposed next assessment, not a condition retroactively imposed on S10. A separate prospective comparison would need prelaunch enrollment, a matched baseline and full packet/refresh/review cost accounting; D is retrospective operational evidence. The current parallel S2 cost probe supplies only procedure-specific assessment headroom and no manual S10 savings estimate. The source evidence [manifest](/home/bline/.local/state/work-engine/manual-decomposition-s10-20261020/source-evidence-manifest-20261021-v2.json) binds this proposal's source revisions. Current use excludes the withdrawn S2 F3 absence claims, while preserving the source author's completed F3 remediation chain and invariant 6's separate INSUFFICIENT basis. The skill's evidence guidance addresses the general failure: a missing statement in a downstream summary does not prove its referenced inputs lacked that evidence.
