## 1. Decision and feasibility result

Retain the existing `retained-repository-analysis-v1` task host, task owner and lifecycle. Select its existing **native ChatGPT access-lease realization with the pinned Linux mount/network isolation profile** as the proposed first production realization; `control: native` remains the selection. This is a route proposal, not a currently qualified deployment or authorization to consume a lease. Do not add a general supervisor, inference executor, research evaluator or alternate platform.

There are two different unmet conditions:

1. **Production authority:** an explicit operator installer can admit an exact immutable installation through the existing trusted startup boundary, with authority material outside the native child's mounted root and tool surface. The necessary engineering is bounded and specified below. Authentic owner/delegation decisions and independent evidence of the realized installation have not been supplied. Source inspection, local file ownership and configured labels cannot supply those facts.
2. **Actual A5 delivery:** the inspected native integration has send-intent, tool-result and history evidence, but no established observation of a complete post-compilation model request or its actual model-visible tool payload. The selected access-lease route uses an opaque HTTPS CONNECT proxy. A5's complete-card delivery claim is therefore **unestablished**, and its required native producer capability is a blocking dependency for that integration. The proposed observation interface below is not evidence that the pinned native producer implements it.

The smallest next engineering proposal is **M4c-b: the bounded installed-authority loader and producer admission**, with successful production preparation still unavailable when actual delivery is missing. It can qualify its mechanics without issuing research standing, running A5 or admitting the audit. A5 integration requires a separately accepted native observation capability and budget; a forged or synthetic delivery record cannot bridge that dependency. Sections 4–6 make the full intended integration reviewable now without treating the missing capability as an implementation detail.

## 3. P1 — installed authority and admitted operations

### 3.1 Owners and trust root

The human/research owner owns qualification; the decision owner owns applicability for the exact audit use. Either may explicitly delegate the appropriate role. The operator/startup owner installs those decisions and capability scopes, but does not acquire either research role by being the local UID or installer. One person may hold several roles only when the actual decisions establish that fact. Lifecycle execution/inference grants remain with their existing owners.

Select a narrow initial route: an **explicit operator installer**, outside the advisory/audit native actors, installs a finite allowlist of exact operation intents. No automated publication watcher or generic remote issuer protocol is needed for this first use. The installer retains actual authenticated owner/delegation sources and separate attributable admissions for upstream research authority and downstream exact operations. Together these bind the authority installation, approval-event pins, issuer-to-role/delegation relationships, exact purpose/request/decision revision/disclosure scope, nominated task/subject/key, exact producer operations and artifact scope. The record separation in §3.2 prevents an operation from depending on an installation that hashes that same operation. It records who admitted those bytes through which existing owner channel, with the source identity and any authentication limitations. Merely copying a conversation quotation, `issuer`, `owner`, `authority_ref`, signature-looking string or approval filename is not such evidence.

**Outstanding owner prerequisite O1:** the operator must name the actual authenticated owner channel/installer actor and supply the corresponding approval/delegation sources. This packet does not select an unimplemented authentication protocol or authorize a model to attest its own grant. If the proposed manual installer cannot establish that source-to-installation binding, production authority remains unavailable. Adding signatures or another authority service is a separate route/owner decision, not an automatic scope expansion.

The existing trusted startup is the authority entry, not the ordinary task socket. The installer produces an immutable upstream authority installation and its admission receipt, then a separate operation manifest and its admission receipt once the exact basis and operation payloads exist; the protected final startup pins the applicable records. Replacing a protected startup under a separately authorized operator action is a new installation, not a native mutation. An installer receipt is consumed only under that established outer startup admission; its deserialized fields alone never mint a permit.

The existing `installation_digest` and `configuration_digest` thus identify the **applicable upstream authority and admission configuration**, not an earlier deployment and not the final startup that authorizes an operation containing that basis. This is an explicit production meaning for those fields, not a rewrite of controlled fixture bytes or a change to existing digests. Production checks require the referenced artifacts' declared kinds and exact bytes, and the current loaded `I`/`C` applicability join. Retain full current `S`/selection/realization/caller evidence separately; the upstream basis fields do not substitute for it. If that current evidence is unavailable, production admission is unavailable even when `B` is structurally valid.

The complete domain payload is what an operation actually asks the owner to do. For preparation it binds key, expected prior preparation head, exact checked-basis digest and required material references. For adoption it is the existing complete `ResearchAdopt` value: submit, key, expected preparation revision and prepared-basis digest, hashed by its existing codec. Installed role/caller evidence is an outer admission consequence, not another caller-controlled field added to that domain payload. Otherwise hashing the admitting `O` or `S` back into the payload would recreate the cycle. An exact operation remains unauthorized until the loaded protected startup and real `R_O` admit that payload within `I`'s role/key scope.

Record IDs, task IDs, command IDs and an optionally preallocated selection ID are locators, not content digests or grants. An ID may be allocated before the bytes it will name, but such a reference establishes no content or admission claim until the actual retained bytes and checked digest/relationship exist. Content-digest references in this construction never name a future record. Reusing an ID with changed bytes conflicts. No ID equality is used to hide a future/self-dependent digest.

**Constructible preparation/adoption example (design evidence only).** Fix one exact request/key `K`, approved submit `V` with command ID `audit-submit-1`, approved context/snapshot/build/tool configuration and all bounded upstream research, qualification, applicability, A4, actual delivery and attributed-recommendation bytes required by a valid M4b basis. These are input bytes, not promised future outputs. This example assumes O1/C1 evidence has been supplied for construction; neither is established by the example. Let `H` mean the existing exact-byte artifact SHA-256 and let `RB`/`RA` mean the unchanged M4b `research_digest("basis", B)` / `research_digest("adopt", A)` operations. Domain/version codecs continue to be selected by their existing owners.

Every digest operand is fully defined before it is hashed. The input set may have arbitrary already-valid bounded content, but this seven-step construction needs no hash fixed point or unmaterialized receipt. It does not predict an actual lifecycle command or bypass a currentness check. An earlier notice or competing preparation makes the example's comparison fail; it does not authorize changing `A`, rebuilding a grant/command or silently admitting a replacement manifest.

**Adverse construction/control:** substituting `H(S)` for `B.configuration_digest` is invalid: production expects the exact upstream `C` artifact kind and current configuration join, not arbitrary retained bytes. Loading `S2` with changed issuer, snapshot, root, provider/protection or other upstream setting against the same `C` refuses before producer/nomination mutation. Changing `b` changes `p`/`a`; the old `O`/`R_O` then cannot authorize either payload. Missing current `S`/caller/operation evidence cannot be repaired by showing only historical `I`/`C` hashes. Qualification must exercise these refusals and retain the original outcome/evidence; no runtime control was run here.

The acyclic order is `I → R_I → C → B → (P,A) → O → R_O → S → realized startup/caller and operation/outcome evidence`. Existing domain exact-byte codecs, M4b basis/adoption payload identities and lifecycle command codecs remain unchanged. New companion admission evidence is an additive task-owner responsibility, with any required migration preserving existing store/task/command/effect bytes.

Apply M4's existing limits: identifiers at most 128 UTF-8 bytes, at most 256 dependency pins / 128 artifact members, metadata at most 128 KiB, retained basis artifacts at most 8 MiB, and existing 128 KiB RPC / 8 KiB text or locator bounds. Limit the upstream authority installation to one nominated audit key and the two role-specific approval events for that exact use; its separately admitted operation manifest has at most 16 exact operation intents. A larger operation set requires another bounded installer disposition; do not silently truncate it. Oversized, duplicate-key, unsupported, missing or mismatched inputs refuse before publication. Retain immutable bytes through the existing owner artifact machinery; no arbitrary worktree/network fetch occurs inside a preparation/adoption transaction.

### 3.3 Protection claim and its evidence

The claim consumer is production research admission, before `Installation`, `ResearchProducer` or `VerifiedBasis` becomes usable. Its subject is one exact selected default host/build/config/installation and its admitted native realization. The covered state is installation/approval/intent/artifact integrity and the exclusion of native actor access to producer/install operations. This is not system-wide hostile same-UID isolation.

The installation/artifact roots must be disjoint from the native writable root, approved snapshot members, code-mode companion paths, proxy/relay descriptors, task/lifecycle sockets and any path/FD available through a tool or descendant. The snapshot allowlist exposes approved research/request/card data to the treatment advisory task; it never exposes writable installation material, credentials, producer capabilities, unapproved owner sources or the baseline's excluded research. Observe actual process and namespace identities, mount/FD maps, child tree, effective configuration and installed dynamic tool schema, bound to exact native/helper/bridge/proxy/build bytes. The actor's own report cannot independently attest this grant.

The namespace is an available mechanism, not proof that its current realization meets this claim. Qualify default-build startup and a harmless adversarial native actor attempting forbidden file/socket/producer access; keep the observing host outside the acting child and retain actual refusal/failure outcomes. Verify mounted parent/root aliases, symlink and descriptor reachability, not only lexical paths. Reject an authority/artifact root beneath any mounted writable ancestor; reopen retained artifacts through pinned descriptors or equivalent checked immutable custody rather than following a caller path after validation. Hostile operator processes sharing the trusted host UID, a compromised trusted installer/host/kernel and unobserved outside publication changes remain explicit exclusions. A failure to realize the declared native capability boundary is **unestablished protection**, never qualified production installation.

No runtime assertion is established in this planning pass. Production success additionally needs actual authenticated owner decisions and admissible installation/caller evidence; synthetic issuer/installer fixtures, even against a default executable, establish engineering behavior only.

## 4. P2 — actual consumer delivery and its blocking capability

Use separate advisory and audit subjects/tasks and native contexts/attempts. Proposed locators remain `research-lifecycle-h2-advice-1` and `research-lifecycle-h2-audit-1`; neither is created here. The advisory receives the full exact audit-use request, target/context, alternatives/rubric and qualified current A4 card. A later audit consumes an accepted recommendation and the checked basis through M0's task-input-adoption boundary. An A5 result cannot create adoption, accept itself, grant execution or change research standing.

### 4.1 Exact observation locus

Select the strongest clear locus for this realization: the **native producer's final serialized model request after context compilation, code-mode presentation and truncation, immediately at its provider transport**, with attributable send/acceptance outcome. For tool-based card delivery, observe the actual resulting model-visible tool-output content there, not the host's upstream `send_result`. Observe the complete request that includes the complete card and the exact request/card/reconstruction associations. Preserve the actual payload bytes, not just a digest or a model assertion. Full-card presence in the prepared native prompt, snapshot artifact, operation receipt, thread history or a completed tool-call notification is insufficient.

The observer must bind native binary/protocol/profile/config identity; host incarnation; advisory task/subject; lifecycle context, logical delivery/attempt and physical native segment; native session/thread/turn; model-request ID/sequence; relevant tool/call/bridge-output identity; complete payload artifact digest/length/codec; exact byte spans and decoding that recover request/card/reconstruction; compilation/truncation state; observer identity/custody; provider acceptance/send outcome and its evidence coordinate. Record which observation precedes the retained attributed result. Multiple requests in an advisory turn remain distinct; a later replacement/rebase does not inherit an earlier episode's delivery. A copied observer receipt without its subject/attempt and retained payload correspondence is ineligible.

**Blocking capability C1:** the pinned native app-server integration currently has no established producer-supported final-request observation/acknowledgment contract. `Runner::run_turn` at line 549 observes pre-compilation RPC input; `Runner::tool` and `NativeHarness::send_result` observe a response to the code-mode runtime. A bridge script can discard the returned JSON rather than make it model-visible. Existing history validation and `thread/readState/changed` do not expose that missing distinction. HTTPS CONNECT observation sees network route/traffic, not decrypted final model input. No inspection in this packet establishes an already usable alternative hook.

Before a P2 implementation is admitted, the native adapter/producer owner must supply an exact supported observation hook and schema, the pinned producer build that emits it, payload/sequence/acknowledgment semantics, and evidence that it observes the chosen locus rather than an earlier buffer. Read-only local schema/source evidence may settle that prerequisite; an actual native experiment requires its own authority. If the hook requires changing/requalifying the external native producer, that is a concrete separate owner dependency. Do not substitute TLS interception, an arbitrary JSON receipt callback, the direct semantic-control provider, a new executor or another platform without new scope/owner acceptance. The direct control route is a compile/verify realization, not the selected A5 domain-task consumer.

### 4.2 Owner/API and storage boundary once C1 is established

The native adapter owns observation acquisition and integrity, lifecycle owns native identity/custody and persistence, the task/research host owns the cross-domain delivery-to-card/recommendation join. Proposed owned additions are:

| Owner | Proposed interface / consequence |
| --- | --- |
| `lifecycle-codex` protocol/transport | Decode the exact qualified producer observation into non-deserializable `ObservedCompiledInput`, after checking producer source/coordinates, schema, completeness and retained bytes. Native task text cannot manufacture one. The concrete hook/decoder is **blocked on C1**, not frozen by this name. |
| `lifecycle-service` native runner/actor | Bind that observation to the existing exact turn/attempt/segment and send its durable observation to the existing lifecycle owner. Do not mark delivery from `PrepareTurn`, RPC success, tool dispatch or final text. |
| `lifecycle-store` native persistence | Retain immutable payload and observation with the native identity linkage and exact replay/conflict semantics. Reuse artifact/raw-ingress custody where its established semantics suffice; any necessary owner-owned table/migration is separately reviewed and preserves existing store/turn/effect bytes. No second turn scheduler or inference invocation is introduced. |
| `work-engine-task-host` research integration | `bind_advisory_delivery(observed_input, exact_a4_use) -> VerifiedConsumerDelivery`; then `retain_advisory_recommendation(exact_task_result, delivery, dependencies) -> VerifiedRecommendation`. Both are non-authority evidence. Production basis construction requires them plus the admitted installer/preparer. |

The new strict `research-consumer-delivery-v1` record carries the identities and retained artifact references above. Its outcome is one tagged state: `complete`, `refused`, `unavailable`, `unknown`, or `mismatch`. Only `complete` has a qualified complete-payload reference and exact A4 join. Refusal records declared unsupported/capacity/omitted/truncated conditions; unavailable means observation acquisition failed; unknown means a send/read/acceptance outcome is unresolved; mismatch means bytes, identities or result association conflict. Preserve distinct safe reasons and raw evidence under protected custody. Never turn one of those states into `absent`, an empty complete input, permission to invoke again, or an eligible recommendation.

`complete` requires both complete matching bytes and attributable evidence that this payload crossed the declared consumer boundary. Serialization-only or unacknowledged send evidence remains unknown. Explicit truncation, missing card spans, invalid/duplicate fields, ambiguous encodings, missing chunks or out-of-order/mismatched coordinates cannot satisfy completeness. A result that names the correct digest but is attributed to another task/context/attempt or different payload is mismatch. A result/refusal without eligible delivery remains a retained advisory outcome, never a prepared production basis. Lost replies are resolved only through existing owner/native operation readback; absence of a response authorizes no reinvocation or context/grant/basis rebase.

The immutable dependency order remains: upstream request/research/approvals and applicable authority installation/admission configuration → A4 card/reconstruction → observed delivered payload/receipt → attributed recommendation → prepared basis → exact operation intents/manifest and protected startup/caller admission → adoption/input/saved lifecycle command → later execution/verification. The full record split and current-startup join are defined in §3.2; downstream startup/operation evidence is retained outside the earlier basis. Do not insert downstream records into the upstream manifest or hash future receipts. Preserve M4b's unique dependency-map decoder and changed-input/new-recommendation invalidation semantics. If the actual full request exceeds the 8 MiB retained-basis ceiling or any stricter native/envelope bound, refuse capacity; changing that ceiling requires an explicit bounded storage disposition.
