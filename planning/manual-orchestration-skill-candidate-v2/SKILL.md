# Manual orchestration

Use model judgment to divide an authorized objective into coherent outcomes whose evidence and ownership can be checked. Keep the accepted objective, authority, source identity, unresolved work and review requirements visible. Packet precision is not proof that all obligations were found; test the proposed decomposition against the owning contract and relevant boundaries before dispatch and when new facts appear. The route, slice size and order may change when evidence warrants it.

Load only the role needed now: [coordinator](references/coordinator.md) for decomposition, dispatch and integration; [supervisor](references/supervisor.md) for a delegated engineering group, validation and review collection; [worker](references/worker.md) for one coherent bounded assignment. Consult [demonstrated forms](references/demonstrated-forms.md) when a concrete pilot example helps form or interpret an artifact. Repository instructions and owning contracts still govern each role.

Separate worker completion, unfinished continuation, supervisor acceptance, private readiness, and root integration. Carry exact source, subject, binary/schema and evidence identity where those affect the claim; preserve failures and uncertainty. Refresh the *meaning* of affected claims when source or assumptions change, not just their hashes. Record requested model/effort separately from observed identity, and leave unavailable measurements unknown. The coordinator retains responsibility for collecting required child results and owned processes; use an explicit follow-up for a retained agent and a fresh launch for new context.

This is a manual coordination adapter. In a configured Slice Builder campaign, use its owning [skill and receipt contract](../../skills/slice-builder/SKILL.md) unless that contract's owner accepts compatible continuation semantics. An unfinished manual `handoff_ready` is not its terminal `audit_receipt`, `handoff_receipt`, or gate success. The skill grants no new mutation, provider, review, acceptance or publication authority.
