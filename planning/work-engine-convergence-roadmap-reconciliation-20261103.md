# Work Engine convergence roadmap reconciliation

Prepared 2026-11-03 for the maintainer. Strategic verdict: **revise the delivery
route around demonstrated root behavior and existing Rust semantic owners**.
The objective and governing doctrine remain sound. The next useful proof is a
bounded composition with recoverable work and explicit authority, rather than
more subsystem construction justified only by the planned architecture.

[machine-readable strategic handoff](work-engine-convergence-strategic-handoff-20261103.json)
records the verdict, evidence, dependencies, authority needs and reconsideration

remain decisions for their owners. Existing campaign grants are not reassigned

## Proposed strategic route

The following is the proposed replacement direction for the roadmap's current
priority list, subject to owner adoption:

1. **Use the root workflow as the operating baseline.** Preserve demonstrated
   objective interpretation, bounded ownership, model-selected decomposition,
   separate review, useful continuity and truthful terminal reporting. The
   reference is observable behavior under its contracts. Human authority and
   required outcomes continue to come from their owning contracts; operational
   success cannot authorize weaker requirements.
2. **Establish one supported execution profile.** Reuse current lifecycle and
   native-launch work to bind actual capabilities, startup inputs, evidence
   producers and recovery limits. A profile with insufficient evidence returns
   an unsupported or unestablished result. A smaller claim needs the authority
   that owns the affected acceptance condition.
3. **Compose one complete workflow from existing owners.** Identify the exact
   admitted task, logical roles, authority source, process/result collectors,
   delivery identities and domain readbacks. Give each effect one owning route.
   Root reasoning uses the owners' operations and observations; the root's
   conversation is replaceable working memory.
4. **Demonstrate recovery and authority preservation on useful work.** Replace
   the supervisor while a child is outstanding and exercise an uncertain
   submission or owner-commit boundary. Recover pending decisions, exact
   results, applicable review and current authority without treating unknown
   effects as safe retries. The chosen task must actually fit the qualified
   operation profile.
5. **Measure the accepted consequence.** Complete the qualified host/manual
   comparison under its own protocol and measure the integrated workflow's
   total cost separately. A recovery or authority improvement may justify a
   cost increase; its protected consequence and tradeoff should be explicit.
6. **Expand from demonstrated demand.** Broader operations, providers,
   organization automation and operator surfaces earn further implementation
   through a named consumer and an unmet consequence. Complete the existing
   standalone release requirements under their own acceptance scope.

These are priority and dependency recommendations. Independent work can
continue where its accepted scope and prerequisites permit. They do not impose
a universal task sequence, a fixed agent topology or a requirement to keep
Work Engine running while its replacement is built.

Additional construction of a generalized organization compiler, standing
supervisory layers, speculative shared workflow frameworks, unused extension
activation, broad backend interchangeability or a general OS observer should
not sit on this vertical's critical path without a concrete required
consequence. This is a scope recommendation, not a retirement of accepted
capabilities or separately authorized work. Extension runtime already has a
[consumer-based deferral](../records/rust-compiler-c5-plan.md); the existing
compiler remains useful.

## Next reviewable integration milestone

The proposed outcome is **one root-directed engineering task with recoverable
execution through the selected Rust owners**. Before an engineering grant, its
contract needs a named integration owner and a defensible workload-to-capability
match. A small deterministic-helper change with an existing behavioral check
is a candidate workload, not a claim that current snapshot-only operations can
perform mutation.

| Required consequence | Evidence needed at acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Work fits the admitted scope | Exact task, approved operation profile, applicable authority and observed effective capabilities. Missing builder mutation or review capability remains an unmet dependency. |
| The supervisor is replaceable | The successor recovers the objective, current organization/assignments, pending authority, exact child identity and collection responsibility from owned durable state, without depending on the predecessor's conversation. |
| Effects retain identity under interruption | A selected uncertain-start or pre/post-owner-commit cut is reconciled through its owner. A lost acknowledgment cannot authorize duplicate work; different stores are not assumed to share a transaction. |
| Review remains applicable | Exact subject, required independence evidence, findings, remediation lineage and acceptance-owner disposition remain available through recovery. |
| Completion crosses the right authority boundary | An authorized acceptance is recorded separately from execution and review. If Git publication is part of the selected task, use its separately admitted guarded path; otherwise report an accepted private result without claiming publication. |
| Cost and failure are observable | Report root and all-actor cost, repairs, elapsed time, unresolved tails and operator intervention where observable, alongside accepted/failed/unsupported outcomes. |

This outcome can be built and qualified offline in a fresh composition,
consistent with [Rust development direction](../docs/rust-development.md).
Continuous availability, dual writers and a temporary Node dispatcher are not
default requirements. Any bridge needs a concrete consumer, bounded need and
removal condition. Existing external development tools remain available for
building and recovering the system; self-hosting can expand after qualification.

If the existing profile supports only the retained-snapshot task, report that
bounded result under its accepted claim. It does not satisfy the proposed
engineering-task milestone. Return the missing operation/authority boundary
and an integration estimate to the owner instead of silently narrowing the
milestone or opening an unbounded backend project.

## Adoption and reconsideration

The immediate decision is whether to adopt this route as strategic guidance.
The next engineering decision is the selected workflow's exact scope, supported
capability profile, integration owner, authority and total validation/recovery
budget. Existing approvals are reusable where they actually cover the same
subject; this handoff requests no renewal of already granted work.

`roadmap.md` is a work-source input for
`campaigns/roadmap.yaml`, `campaigns/persistent-runtime-state.yaml` and
`campaigns/persistent-runtime-state-route-open.yaml`. Before publishing the
proposed route into that file, its owner needs to establish the applicable
campaign state and amendment consequences. This planning result provides the
concrete proposal without changing those work-source bytes or active grants.

Reconsider the recommendation when a selected profile qualifies or stops;
isolated-launch qualification or a valid paired pilot changes the economic
evidence; a joined runtime exposes a missing owner; event-delivery feasibility
changes the notification assumptions; the actual admitted capabilities cannot
support the selected engineering task; or the full remaining outcome no longer
fits its explicitly granted resources. These are decision events, not an
automatic schedule or a fixed escalation ladder.
