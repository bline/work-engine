## Agreed direction

### Evidence and time
1. Evidence has inherent, multidimensional uncertainty.
2. Historical observations and present-day applicability are different things.
3. Evidence may become less applicable as time, environments and dependencies change.
4. Questionable, contradictory, aging and refuted evidence stays in the durable research record.
5. Evidence assessments are revisioned. Historical observations are not silently rewritten.

### Distinct judgments
6. Current evidence quality, decision-specific applicability, applicability authorization and execution authority
   are four distinct things.
7. An invalid research register produces no A4 evidence card. The owner stated this as approved D-6 in the
   2026-11-11 closure authorization; the earlier A4 decision-status table (R-2 reconcile, `a4-decision-status.md`
   row D-6) lists it as reserved, and is superseded on this point.
8. A4 must expose the relevant evidence and the material uncertainty without granting authority.

### Decisions, authority and feedback
9. Supervisors use evidence to recommend actions under uncertainty.
10. A separate authority controls execution and spending.
11. Verified execution outcomes become *candidate* research evidence, not automatically accepted conclusions.
12. The intended end-to-end system supports:
    research → assessment → decision → authorization → execution → verification → research feedback.

### First milestone
13. One real, governed, research-informed execution with a reconstructible decision basis.
