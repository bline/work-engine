## Ownership and placement

Keep four boundaries. The producer owns its final transport records and outcomes. `lifecycle-codex` owns acquisition and decoding from the actual owned producer. The existing service actor owns lifecycle association; `lifecycle-store` owns immutable retention and replay/conflict semantics. The task/research host later owns the A4/recommendation join; retained-task-workflow continues to own preparation, adoption and historical basis semantics.

This placement follows the inspected implementation:

- `NativeHarness::spawn_with_access_isolation` checks the official `399ff69c…` pin and constructs the owned child with `env_clear`; `NativeStdio` owns bounded pipes and child collection. Generic runtime framing has no model-request or research meaning.
- `Runner::run_turn` obtains `PrepareTurn`, binds native `turn/start` identity, consumes terminal events and retains exact final history. Those are suitable association points, but RPC success and final history are earlier/different evidence from compiled input.
- `native_delivery_binding` already joins native invocation to input, delivery, effect, queue attempt, native attempt, segment and context. `native_final_material` separately retains final text and its history-ingress coordinate. Neither should be renamed compiled-input evidence.
- The native RPC ingress record is bounded to1MiB. Reuse its session/sequence identity principles, not its RPC envelope or payload ceiling for an8MiB final request. Reuse the store's staged/synced artifact publication for payload bytes.

Putting the whole feature in the task host would duplicate native process/custody knowledge and encourage accepting caller receipts. Putting it in retained-task-workflow would combine research authority with provider evidence and disturb the schema-3 basis boundary. A standalone evidence service or universal trace platform adds no necessary owner. Keeping it only as a parser would leave the named durable lifecycle association unestablished. The chosen unit therefore crosses adapter/service/store as one joined outcome, without adding another scheduler or authorization service.

## Data and authority flow

1. **Bind custody at execution.** A provider-disabled test composition names the exact repaired native source/build and companion bytes, protocol/profile/config, observer build and confinement. The runner creates a fresh owned capture location before spawn and retains its root/handle identity with the native session and host incarnation. Acquisition accepts only that location and owned launch, never a task-supplied path, caller PID, copied manifest or `qualified=true`. The test path checks the exact frozen composition and is unavailable in the ordinary library/startup path; the official production pin remains unchanged. The current adapter does not expose this private-native launch route, so its test-only realization is part of the unit's cost and review. If existing confinement cannot support it without a new production/isolation contract, stop with that prerequisite.
2. **Validate untrusted records and bytes.** Bound envelope, record count, cumulative bytes and decoding before allocation; retain the raw producer bytes. Preserve the128KiB metadata ceiling and the repaired producer's selected bound, never above the8MiB combined retained-payload ceiling; declare finite per-turn acquisition limits in the implementation packet. Reject duplicate fields before parsing into ordinary values, unknown kind/version/shape, duplicate representations, malformed IDs, unsafe/aliased/symlink payload paths, changed source handles, unsupported encoding, decompression excess and length/digest/decoded-wire disagreement. Check the complete self-contained request representation and every inference-call/physical-send coordinate; retain explicit rejection, retry, fallback and terminal outcomes. No logical-replay object, declared hash, `self_contained` flag or fixture evidence boolean substitutes for the accepted producer semantics plus checked bytes and custody. The decoder cannot call inference or create a new turn.
3. **Join facts in their existing owner.** The actor resolves IDs from its actual session and store-owned turn entry, not from labels in the capture. Match subject, context generation, host incarnation, selected native composition/profile/config, native thread/turn, invocation and lifecycle attempt. For domain work join the original FIFO input/delivery/effect/queue attempt/native segment; keep every model request and physical send distinct. Bind publication and crossing/provider evidence to that same request and to the successful exact native outcome required by the repaired contract. Unknown or failed native outcome cannot qualify merely because completion-shaped trace bytes exist. Control/rehydration observations cannot acquire a domain-work delivery association. Replacement or historical readback never rebases evidence onto a new context or attempt.
4. **Retain through lifecycle custody.** Copy checked bytes into the lifecycle artifact owner, preserving original producer SHA-256/length/codec alongside the lifecycle artifact identity; the two codec identities need not be equal. Publish synced artifact bytes before committing observation references. One lifecycle transaction validates the referenced native rows and all observation associations, then commits the immutable observation/child-request references. An interrupted artifact publication may leave only an unreferenced owned artifact; an interrupted observation commit must leave no qualified reference. A missing capture must not rewrite an already observed native settlement or authorize execution again. Identical replay returns the original record; changed bytes/outcome at an existing coordinate produces a retained conflict/ineligible result, never replacement. Failed/rejected/unknown evidence remains attributable diagnostic state.
5. **Read evidence without granting authority.** A read-only store accessor returns retained bytes, coordinates, tagged outcome and qualification scope after restart. It does not emit `research-consumer-delivery-v1:complete`, construct `VerifiedBasis`, grant a producer role or mutate a research key. No new external wire protocol is required for this internal unit. The later task-host join consumes these facts through the owning lifecycle read boundary; it cannot accept a caller-provided receipt in their place.

An acquisition failure is unavailable; unsupported/capacity/explicit omission is refused; unresolved send/publication/native outcome is unknown or failed as observed; conflicting bytes/identities are mismatch. No state becomes an empty complete request, permission to retry, remote settlement, or absence of an effect. Existing owner/native readback is the recovery route. Later historical evidence remains readable when fresh use is fenced.
