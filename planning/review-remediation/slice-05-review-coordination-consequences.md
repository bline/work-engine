# Slice 5 — Reframe Review Coordination Around Evidence Validity

## Protected consequence

Independent review remains bound to the exact code, assumptions, and downstream
consequence it evaluated. Before that review is used for acceptance or another
consequential decision, the system establishes that it is still applicable or
truthfully records why it was refreshed, superseded, or composed with later
work.

## Observed doctrine conflict

The durable-review proposal and roadmap turn mutation-scope reservation into a
general prerequisite for later slice mutation. They require plan acceptance,
route reopening, repair, and remediation to consult a queue and acquire or
revise reservations across files and semantic relationships.

Immutable Git-backed checkpoints already preserve the exact review snapshot.
Later workspace mutation does not retroactively change what was reviewed. It
may make that review insufficient for a later integrated state, but waiting,
re-reviewing, composing, rebasing, superseding, or proving independence are
contextual routes—not one invariant reservation procedure.

## Acceptance consequences

- Review evidence names an immutable subject and its material assumptions.
- Use of that evidence against a later state includes an applicability/freshness
  judgment proportionate to the consequence.
- Confirmed conflicts cannot be hidden by claiming the old review covers the
  new state.
- The machinery may expose queue, overlap, snapshot, reservation, and
  supersession capabilities with clear costs and limitations.
- Runtime doctrine does not require global mutation reservations unless an
  identified concurrency or authority contract makes them causally necessary.
- Unrelated work is not blocked merely because semantic non-overlap cannot be
  exhaustively proven.
- Any retained deterministic blocking rule names the concrete invalid state it
  prevents and remains route-invariant.

## Required vertical proof

Demonstrate at least three consequences: a later non-conflicting change that can
continue without weakening review truth; a conflicting integrated change that
cannot reuse stale review evidence; and a delayed review against its immutable
snapshot whose result remains truthful even though the working tree later
changed.

## Insufficient substitutes

- Replacing file locks with a larger fixed semantic-overlap taxonomy.
- Treating every queue revision as a conflict.
- Removing snapshot identity or review provenance.
- Keeping mandatory reservations but labeling them “guidance.”

## Strategic effect

Roadmap language should state the evidence-validity and coordination
consequences. Reservation machinery may remain a candidate implementation or
future affordance until evidence establishes that it adds necessary reach.
