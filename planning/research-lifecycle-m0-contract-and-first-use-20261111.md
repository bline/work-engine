## 2. LC-1 — exact research subject and authority scope

Use a conservative legacy-finding subject: **finding ID plus the exact study
blob from the pinned register commit**. This binds proposition, conditions,
values, limitations and sources without pretending the existing stable ID is
versioned meaning. A study edit creates a new subject even if unrelated text
changed; that conservative cost is acceptable for the first case.

A new applicability disposition binds that subject, the exact registered decision
revision and audit-request digest, its relation and bounds, and attributable owner
authorization evidence. A string naming an owner or `authorization.at` value
is not proof that the actor granted it. The owning trusted admission boundary
establishes the authorization from a saved user decision or delegated grant.

An assessment update of the same subject preserves the old authorization's
identity and bounds. It can change whether the required qualification holds;
it neither revokes nor widens the authorization. A changed subject, decision
revision or request scope needs its own applicability disposition. A5 may propose one but may
not publish approval on behalf of the owner.

For this case, the new disposition can authorize **limited support for an audit**:
recorded compaction behavior and known limitations justify checking the policy.
It must not authorize the claim that H2 is optimal, that Astra was measured
under the limit, or that the historical result predicts the current host.
The replay is contextual evidence about a modelled restart cost, not confirmed
support for a threshold or a fresh launch's floor.

## 3. LC-2 — assessment, qualification and conflict

The research owner admits attributed assessment candidates. Each assessment
binds its subject, evidence bytes/revisions, assessor, as-of context, limitations
and predecessor/correction links. The eight v0.2 uncertainty facets distinguish
`not_assessed` from an assessed `unknown`; qualitative results carry a reason
and evidence. No confidence percentage or universal age penalty is introduced.

For the first case, an assessment author reviews the exact sources and scope;
a separately established reviewer challenges the assessment and source links.
The user or explicitly delegated research acceptance owner admits the qualified
assessment for the audit purpose. A model cannot appoint that authority.
Preserve the reviewer identity, exposure boundary and host-observed capability
configuration required for the independence claim. A reviewer self-description
alone does not establish the grant. If the available host cannot establish a
required independence property, qualification remains unestablished.

Qualification means the declared evidence and limitations are adequate for
this bounded analytical use. It is not acceptance of a general performance
claim or completed independent R-2 acceptance. Existing legacy judgments remain
attributed historical inputs; their `standing`/`currency` words do not silently
satisfy the new qualification contract.

Credible unresolved opposition remains visible. A4 does not choose a winner by
timestamp, majority or a numeric score. For this case, A5 may recommend the audit
with the disagreement exposed; a recommendation relying on a disputed premise
as established needs an explicit qualification disposition resolving that use.

## 4. LC-3 — preserve what was actually used

A contemporaneous basis records the exact request, register and research bundle,
delivered card bytes/digest, selected assessment and authorization revisions,
context check and policy revision, recommendation, and eventual grant/admission/
execution/verification references. It records **what reached the consumer**,
not only what the projection could have delivered.

Keep three products distinct: the original basis; later historical association;
and current reassessment. A later authorization of an old finding is associated
with the old decision when explicitly recorded, but is never inserted into the
old basis as if available then. Existing H2 authorization is historical context
for this new audit, not evidence that this audit was authorized in 2026-11-09.

Append corrected records and reconsideration obligations referencing affected
basis IDs. Preserve the original input and assessment bytes. For this one case,
the research owner records the affected basis and the user/decision owner owns
its disposition. Acknowledgment is not completed reconsideration.

The first projection supports **current requests only**, requiring the request
revision to equal that decision's current registered revision in its pinned
bundle. Prior/historical-card requests return an explicit unsupported outcome,
with no card and no fallback to current content. D-8 historical choices stay
reserved; this bounded interface must not claim full legacy A4 conformance.

## 5. LC-4 — currentness at the named consumption boundary

The selected consumption boundary is **admission of the task input containing
the accepted recommendation and research basis**, before it enters lifecycle's
native input stream. A5 recommendation and task publication are distinct events.

The task owner owns a preparation slot keyed by task/request identity. It stores
only admission dependencies, their immutable references and a monotonic
preparation revision; it does not own research judgments or qualification. The
trusted research-publication adapter may invalidate that slot for a new
assessment, applicability or correction revision. The trusted decision/operator
adapter may invalidate it for a target, request, policy or observed context
change. These authorities come from host configuration and authenticated input,
not from a publisher name inside a native task message. Native task operations
cannot mark preparation current or clear an invalidation.

The admission preparation checks exact request, target document, research and
assessment identities; applicability scope; qualification; applicable correction
heads; and known context changes. The context record names model/host and the
provenance or unavailability of compaction-limit/session-inheritance facts.
A historical off-repository configuration value is not a current observation.
For an audit, unknown current conditions are an explicit question the result
must preserve; they do not justify a prediction or a silent assumption.

Known material changes to an input bound by the recommendation make that
recommendation ineligible for this admission until reassessed. This is a scoped
admission result, not automatic revocation of the owner's grant. Unrelated work
can continue. A change to a historical observation's applicability does not
rewrite the observation.

The adopted input binds an immutable prepared basis and the expected preparation
revision. Within the same `retained-task-workflow` transaction that checks this
slot, the task owner records adoption, immutable task input and the exact saved
lifecycle command. Trusted invalidation and adoption serialize through that
owner transaction; a different task revision cannot substitute for the
preparation revision. A notice committed before the atomic adoption check makes
the old comparison fail. A notice serialized after adoption instead records a
reconsideration obligation against the exact adopted basis. “Before” refers to
this owned ordering, not message arrival or an external source's wall clock.

The existing `submit_with_admission` is an implementation seam, not evidence
that these preparation records or notice operations already exist. M4 must add
this bounded task-owner capability and trusted host admission path. A4 remains
read-only and no research publisher gains lifecycle or task-result authority.

A trusted preparer supplies the checked bundle and its dependency revisions to
that slot. It cannot make a stale preparation ready by changing only its digest;
resolution/repreparation binds the changed inputs and a new recommendation when
required. An exact duplicate adoption reads back the saved command and receipt;
it does not adopt against a newer slot. The owner preserves which preparation
revision won and the inputs actually admitted.

Relevant change notices committed through the named producers are the covered
scope. Arbitrary unobserved changes outside it remain a disclosed limitation;
a Git pin does not freeze the outside world. No cross-store atomic transaction
or continuous world-currentness is claimed. The future execution packet names
the actual configured notice sources; absent sources cannot support a claim
that all relevant changes are observed.

## 6. LC-5 — minimum durable records and Rust placement

Choose an **explicitly joined, research-owner companion bundle**, initially at
`metrics/research-lifecycle/`, outside the closed legacy register file set.
Keep the existing catalogue and accepted register validation unchanged. Add the
new typed reader/join validation under `research-register`; A4 receives its
privately constructed validated input and never decides register validity.

The first bundle needs only these semantic records:

| Record | Required distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Finding subject reference | Exact legacy finding and study blob; associated evidence pins |
| Assessment revision | Exact subject, checked scope/facets, evidence, limitations, correction/competition links and qualification evidence |
| Audit-use request | Purpose, alternatives, exact target, operating context, owner and acceptance requirements |
| Applicability disposition | Exact research subject and decision request, relation/bounds and independently established authorization reference |
| Prepared basis and adoption receipt | Separate downstream records of actual delivered inputs/recommendation/checks, then the task-owner adoption and winning preparation revision |
| Outcome/feedback reference | Exact execution/verification identity, costs/failures/unknowns and candidate status |

The companion owns these new records; it does not overwrite legacy judgments
or supply a second canonical value for legacy standing. The selected new-use
profile consumes the explicitly admitted assessment revision. Legacy values
retain their source and scope and remain visible where material.

A bundle manifest binds its schema version and exact path/blob/digest inputs,
including the source snapshot commit containing the legacy register and target
document. The companion is published in a later commit or immutable artifact;
its manifest never embeds its own commit ID. Register validation and in-process
anchors use the same source snapshot, and the join verifies the manifest
register tree against that snapshot. The request and its adopted basis retain
both identities; a companion revision does not masquerade as a source revision. Each input is immutable UTF-8 JSON;
new revision identity is SHA-256 of the exact stored bytes, including newline.
Field-order changes therefore create a new revision. Do not apply this codec
to existing claim, register or coordination identities. Unknown versions,
duplicate identities/keys, dangling links, mismatched pins and cycles in required
predecessor links fail the join. Relative paths cannot escape the owned bundle.

The **initial research-use manifest** contains only upstream subject/evidence,
assessment/qualification, decision request/context and applicability records.
It contains no card, recommendation, prepared/adopted basis or outcome. Contained
records do not embed that manifest's identity. Each reference required for
validation resolves to already published immutable bytes, so the first valid
bundle can exist before projection.

A4 derives the card from that validated bundle. A separate consumer-delivery
record identifies the card that actually reached A5; the recommendation refers
to those existing inputs. A prepared basis then binds that bundle, card,
delivery and recommendation. The task owner's later adoption receipt refers to
the prepared basis and actual admission. Subsequent outcome and reconsideration
records point back to existing basis/receipt identities. Append these downstream
records under their owning domains and companion history; they are not required
members of the upstream manifest and never change its digest. An optional
history index may point to already existing records without becoming a prerequisite
for its indexed inputs. No record's identity depends on its own digest, a future
output, or a manifest that hashes that record. This is causal dependency, not a
required model work sequence.

Publish any manifest only when every named immutable input exists. An incomplete
publication is unavailable, never a partially valid bundle. A new validated
research-use input requires both the original `ValidRegister` proof and the
upstream join proof. Invalid register still means **no card (D-6)**. Unreadable
register and invalid companion are distinct diagnostics, also without a card.

Retain the exact inputs needed to reconstruct every adopted basis; missing
retained bytes make reconstruction unavailable rather than silently fetching a
newer URL. No garbage-collection or archive policy is introduced in this slice.

The selected receipt owners retain grants, input FIFO, task revisions, native
settlement and result publication. Extend their own records with exact basis
references where needed. Avoid a parallel task receipt, generic research
runtime, universal grant type, or dependency on the standalone portfolio or
readiness workspaces merely because they have similarly named records.

## 7. LC-6 — actual consumer and bounded execution

The [task host contract](../docs/rust-retained-task-host-contract.md) is the
existing consumer seam. `SubmitTask` currently carries the objective, engineering
question, approved snapshot and canonical facts. `submit_with_admission` saves
owner input with the exact outbound lifecycle command. The production host's
private `grant_for_context` selects a trusted configured grant by exact context
and scope. Its test-only `grant` helper is not a production grant API.

The adapter must preserve exact approved snapshot/facts and store the research
basis binding without allowing task text to install a grant. Lifecycle continues
to check native admission and task operations. Owner input commit is not native
completion; attributed task result is not independent acceptance.

The audit reads only its approved repository snapshot and supplied trusted facts.
Its result identifies supported, unsupported and unknown parts of the H2 policy,
cites exact evidence, proposes a scoped wording correction or justified no change,
and names what further measurement would be needed. It cannot inspect arbitrary
home directories, run shell measurements, edit files, change startup grants,
deploy skills, or spend on an experiment through this analysis profile.

A future execution packet must supply an actual qualified build/provider/startup,
snapshot manifest, task/subject identities, exact scope/budget and grants, output
limits, acceptance owner, verifier and receipt locations. The existing consumer
is implementation material; this planning pass does not establish current live
readiness or release acceptance. If that prerequisite is not qualified, report
it before admission; do not create a new runtime to bypass it.

snapshot without the added register/research bundle. Freeze the common objective,

uses the same bounded analysis effect profile and acceptance requirements.
A deferral or refusal is an outcome, not an excluded failed run.

This baseline is **usual policy context without added research assistance**.
The policy already contains research-derived text. Record that contamination;
do not call the baseline research-naive or claim to isolate projection formatting
from extra research access. Keep treatment research unavailable to the baseline
through the declared snapshot/tool surface. If it becomes exposed, retain the
run and mark the comparison compromised; do not silently retry for a cleaner
result. The user must accept this baseline's limited estimand for the first case.

Use separate actor contexts with actual exposure evidence; a common author cannot
pretend to be a naive baseline. A verifier, with arm identities hidden where
feasible, evaluates exact source attribution, preserved uncertainty/authority,
policy-scope correctness, useful proposed result and unsupported claims. Record
blinding limits. Cost includes preparation/curation, projection, A5, execution,
verification, repair and coordination, with shared cost shown separately and a
prebound allocation rule. Unpriced or unsettled tail remains unknown. Report
quality and cost separately, plus reasons for non-comparable outcomes.

One pair is a feasibility observation, not causal proof or a calibrated benefit
estimate. Feedback candidates bind execution and verification receipts and the
exposure/acceptance scope. Include refusal, failure and unknown effects, preserve
source execution IDs for deduplication, and route scientific conclusions through
a new assessment/qualification decision. Task success never promotes a claim.
