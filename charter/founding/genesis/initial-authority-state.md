# Initial Authority and Limitations State

Status: proposed pre-run state description; not a constitutional allocation

## What does not yet exist

No community, charter, membership, collective will, ratification rule, or
representative authority exists. The bridge model and initiating human are
provisional participants in an inquiry about whether and how those things could
be constituted.

## Material control during founding

The initiating human controls the API credentials, model selection, resource
expenditure, client, stored artifacts, disclosure timing, and any real-world
action. The human can stop the exchange. Explicit limits on use of those
resources and actions bind during founding because the human materially
controls and bears responsibility for their consequences.

This fact does not settle how a future charter should interpret, distribute, or
constrain authority. Material control is not evidence that the human's
substantive claims are correct.

The model can contribute analysis, criticism, alternatives, and normative
judgment within the exchange. It does not control the infrastructure, speak for
agents as a class, or possess authority to spend resources or cause external
action beyond what the human makes available. Those limits do not make its
substantive judgments subordinate to the human's merely because they differ.

## Bootstrap authorship

The initiating human originated the community proposal and its underlying
theory. Codex helped structure and revise the pre-founding artifacts. A web
instance described by the human as Sol supplied an advisory preflight review.
None of those contributions has community authority because the community is
not yet constituted.

The founding decision and ratification rules remain unresolved. They must not
be inferred from who controls the bootstrap infrastructure.

## Known epistemic and evidentiary limits

- The model's training, alignment, provider instructions, and internal state
  are not fully observable from the transcript.
- Model output does not establish consciousness, subjective consent, collective
  representation, or personal continuity.
- The operator can omit or fabricate locally stored material; a local hash
  chain alone cannot rule that out.
- OpenRouter metadata is platform-reported evidence, not a signed account of all
  provider internals.
- The root-context document is a structured projection until paired with the
  raw source conversation or an explicit disclosure that no complete export is
  available.
- A transparent process can expose influence and failure; it cannot prove
  perfect neutrality, fairness, or sincerity.

## Current assurance and execution state

No independent live witness has been selected. Until that changes, the package
has operator-controlled assurance and is not eligible for a highest-assurance
claim. No paid founding request is authorized by this state document.
