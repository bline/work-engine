# Community Charter Workspace

This directory holds the founding record for a possible enduring community of
human and agent experts.

The community is not yet constituted. No charter has been ratified, no
collective will has been established, and placement in this directory does not
grant founder, member, or representative authority.

## Founding artifacts

- [`founding/root-context.md`](founding/root-context.md) records the purpose,
  tensions, and provenance that led to the founding dialogue.
- [`founding/bridge-agent-seed.md`](founding/bridge-agent-seed.md) is the compact
  seed for the bridge agent that will participate in that dialogue.
- [`founding/bridge-agent-seed-v1.md`](founding/bridge-agent-seed-v1.md)
  preserves the first compact revision and the individual-identity framing it
  used before the initiating human corrected it.
- [`founding/bridge-agent-seed-v2.md`](founding/bridge-agent-seed-v2.md)
  preserves the collective-identity revision before its temporary-authority
  boundary was corrected.
- [`founding/bridge-agent-seed-v0.md`](founding/bridge-agent-seed-v0.md)
  preserves the human-supplied first draft so later revisions do not erase
  their lineage.
- [`founding/genesis-provenance-protocol.md`](founding/genesis-provenance-protocol.md)
  proposes the evidence chain that must exist before the founding run begins.
  It is not active until its exact revision is accepted and committed.
- [`founding/human-theory-contextual-simulation.md`](founding/human-theory-contextual-simulation.md)
  preserves the initiating human's political-philosophy theory and a
  claim-separated interpretation. It is founding input, not community doctrine.
- [`founding/openrouter-launch.md`](founding/openrouter-launch.md) records the
  intended bridge runtime and the provenance needed to know which model and
  provider actually participated.
- [`founding/genesis/`](founding/genesis/) contains the separately bounded
  opening question, runtime manifest, initial authority state, event schema,
  seed-revision mechanics, and current assurance classification.
- [`founding/genesis-manifest.json`](founding/genesis-manifest.json) is the
  machine-readable digest inventory for the exact proposed genesis bundle. Its
  status remains proposed until accepted, committed, and witnessed or expressly
  classified at lower assurance.
- [`founding/reviews/2026-08-31-web-sol-preflight.md`](founding/reviews/2026-08-31-web-sol-preflight.md)
  preserves the user-supplied external preflight review as advisory evidence.
- [`founding/reviews/2026-08-31-web-sol-preflight-remediation.md`](founding/reviews/2026-08-31-web-sol-preflight-remediation.md)
  records the local finding disposition without claiming reviewer acceptance.

A future charter and any community documents should remain visibly distinct
from this pre-charter material. Their authority must come from the founding
process they record, not from this directory structure.
